# Pass 3 · What the weighted evaluator has to preserve

**November 24, 2026.** The contracting argument assumes a small arithmetic circuit, converts it to a weighted evaluator, and rules out an evaluator with the resulting guarantees. This pass checked the circuit conversion, the resource arithmetic at one scale change, and how the formal source supplies the cut hypothesis used in the contradiction.

### Circuit to weighted program

**Source claim.** K.2, p. 56, expands the homogeneous parts of a gate. At a multiplication gate it handles the two degree-zero factors separately. For positive degree i, the other terms split i into two positive degrees. The construction collects chains of steps through the circuit into field coefficients. A nondeterministic run guesses a parse tree with at most d leaves. Summing its contributions over accepting runs gives the value of all parts of degree at most d on a word’s indicator assignment.

**Resource interpretation.** The program may use O((L+n)³) circuit-dependent field coefficients as nonuniform advice. A run records names. Its execution cannot branch on an arbitrary named value. The time guarantee bounds each accepting run, with halting on every input in the stated size domain. An exponential number of accepting runs is compatible with that guarantee. [Model, pp. 48–49 and 55–56](../primary/vp-vnp/paper.pdf#page=48) · [Formal evaluator statement](../primary/vp-vnp/Paper/LowDeg.lean)

**Independent check.** At g=(x+c)² both incoming edges can point to the same gate x+c. The homogeneous terms are c², 2cx and x². There are two chains to the linear term even when the incoming gate is shared. They cancel in characteristic two. Counting distinct gates instead of paths here would give a different polynomial. The included check evaluates the three terms on every element of the prime fields of sizes 2, 3 and 5 with c=1. This illustrates the recursion on a shared gate. It does not test the interpreter. **Domain at the call site.** In K.3, pp. 56–57, take n=v^(H−32)+1 and d=n. The auxiliary polynomial Φ uses an end mark. At the code for a longer word, the end mark excludes the monomial for a proper prefix. The degree bound includes the end mark. Proposition I.1 underwrites the assertion that these values reproduce the universal program on short plain instances. With t=v^(H+1), its required O((|z|+v^H) log²(|z|+v^H)) time fits for large v. The formal construction supplies the degree, evaluation, and circuit-transfer properties used downstream. Exact equality of its polynomial to the displayed word sum is a stronger correspondence question. See Appendix Q, pp. 69–70.
