# Pass 13D · The Hamiltonian-cycle Boolean witness

**Question.** Does the released definition of VNP admit the Hamiltonian-cycle family in every characteristic, with all witnesses counted exactly once and a polynomial bound covering the auxiliary variables? See M.4, printed p. 62. The displayed derivation and resource table are in [the editable report section](../report/pass13d.tex). The [public statement](../primary/vp-vnp/Statement.lean#L76) and [internal definition](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Final/CClasses.lean#L36) both quantify over one VP witness family before evaluating its Boolean auxiliaries. The original matrix variables remain formal indeterminates.

## Construction and independent derivation

For positive n, select an order of the vertices with the n-by-n table Y. For each ordered pair of different cells sharing a row or a column, include the factor 1−YₐYᵦ. Multiply their product by the product of all row sums. A collision now contributes zero termwise in every characteristic. Without collisions a nonempty row selects exactly one vertex, and distinct rows select different vertices. Thus the test takes value 1 on permutation tables and 0 elsewhere. Multiply by Y₀₀ to choose one start, then by a product over i of the sums of YᵢᵤYᵢ₊₁,ᵥ Xᵤᵥ, with i+1 read cyclically. On a rooted table with order π the latter is the monomial ∏ᵢ X_{π(i),π(i+1)}. Given the successor cycle σ, its unique order is π(i)=σⁱ(0). The maps are inverses and preserve each monomial. At n=0 there is one empty table and the constant witness 1. [Order bijection](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Hc/Vnp.lean#L51) · [Witness](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Hc/Vnp.lean#L93) · [Eliminating invalid tables](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Hc/Vnp.lean#L144) · [Polynomial identity](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Hc/Vnp.lean#L189)

There are 2n² variables including the auxiliaries. For n>0 let q=2n²(n−1) be the number of ordered clashes. An m-term product with component circuits of size s costs at most m(s+1)+1 using the formal circuit calculus. The clash factor costs at most 7 and has degree 2. The product of row sums costs 2n²+2n+1 and has degree ≤n. Each edge summand costs 5 and has degree 3, so the product of edge sums costs 6n³+2n+1 and has degree ≤3n. Joining the three products and root bit gives size ≤8q+6n³+2n²+4n+7 and degree ≤2q+4n+1. The source dominates both with 40(n+1)⁴, then by n⁷⁰⁰+700, including n=0 and n=1. The exponent 700 is a convenient common witness to the definition. [Size calculus](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Final/CClasses.lean#L137) · [Quartic bound](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/Hc/Vnp.lean#L221)

## Adversarial controls and verification scope

Removing the root counts n rotations of each cycle for n≥1. For n=2 in characteristic two this gives zero instead of X₀₁X₁₀. Replacing the one-hot constraints with row and column sums equal to 1 in the field admits an all-ones 3-by-3 table over the binary field. These failures arise from different operations. The exact checker enumerates the literal witness over all Boolean tables through n=4 and compares the symbolic multiset to independently enumerated successor cycles. The small cases have 1,1,1,2,6 rooted survivors respectively, including the unit at n=0. The checks concern those finite sizes. The bijection and the polynomial bounds apply to all sizes. [Finite check](../checks/pass13d_witness.py)

The fresh 13-module build through Nc.Hc.Vnp reads back five types and five axiom sets. An additional proof term in the query instantiates the internal VNP definition with the witness. Paper.Fields itself is a downstream module with a larger closure. The M.4 caller uses VP inclusion, this witness, the eventual M.3 bound and its domination of any fixed polynomial circuit-size bound. Under a reverse inclusion, HC would have size at most n^C+C for some C at every n. Take n large enough both for M.3 and for c(log₂ n)^(1/3)≥C+1, with n≥C+2. Then n^(c(log₂ n)^(1/3))≥n^(C+1)≥n^C+C, contradicting the strict circuit lower bound. Membership can therefore carry forward independently of a final judgment on M.3.
