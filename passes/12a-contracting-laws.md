## The hypothesis that H.2 receives

G.2 starts over a contracting field. The Poincaré inequality controls squared distances on all pairs by squared distances on edges at a fixed degree. Cauchy–Schwarz and rounding bound every input load's pairing with a price by the maximum pairing of finitely many edge loads. The maximizing edge load can depend on the price. A.6 is what turns that quantified inequality into a tensor cover. For each rounded input load and sufficiently large power $q$, this cover has at most $2^{\epsilon{}q}$ terms, with each edge-load vector a sum of $q$ admissible edge loads. Choosing $q$ above all finitely many thresholds permits one packet scale $a_1=aq$. The per-layer code length can be $\lambda{}=\lceil \epsilon{}q\rceil+1$. Its contribution to channel load is paid on every layer. Ring restriction uses the subring generated by the finite set of tensor entries. The resulting identity maps to every commutative algebra over that ring. The choice of field, finite laws, gain and degree precedes the network sizes. This is a source reconstruction conditional on F.6 and A.6. ## One move as a finite identity

Fix the earlier outcomes and the current move. Both holders read the same ordering of consumed IDs in each slot. Missing positions are zero on both sides. For $t$ packet positions per slot and $\ell{}\ge{}1$ bits per packet, there are $B=t\ell{}$ layers. In layer $k$, G.1 writes the indicator of agreement at all old slots as a sum over a common law index $i_k$ and a payload $g_{k\nu{}}$ for each channel $\nu{}$. Multiplying the $B$ identities and distributing gives one term for each whole vector $i=(i_k)_k$ and array $g=(g_{k\nu{}})_{k,\nu{}}$, weighted by the product of all local table entries. This preserves multiplicities in the expansion even when distinct terms cancel. At $B=0$ there is one empty choice of index vector and payloads. [Law definition](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/G/Defs.lean#L27) · [Zero padding and agreement](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/G/Move.lean#L62) · [Taking the power](../primary/vp-vnp/Nc/G/Contract.lean#L146)

On channel $\nu{}$, write the successive fixed-width codes of $i_k$, each followed by its payload of known length $L_{i_k\nu{}}$. If the resulting string has length $s_\nu{}$, set $o_\nu{}=\lfloor s_\nu{}/\ell{}\rfloor$ and encode it with a trailing one and zero padding in $o_\nu{}+1$ packets. The final one is the last one in this padded string. Removing it and its padding recovers the raw string, and reading the code at each layer determines the length of the next payload. Thus the shared packet word recovers the full index vector and the channel's payload. Different index vectors can have exactly the same outcome vector. Endpoints seeing the same channel word agree on their decoded indices and payloads. Connectivity propagates agreement on indices to all roles. Choosing the contents on each channel now gives a bijection between consistent expanded local choices and the original global index/payload choices; invalid codewords contribute zero. A possible outcome uses one shared $i_k$ across all channels, so $o_\nu{}=\lfloor\sum_k(\lambda{}+L_{i_k\nu{}})/\ell{}\rfloor$. Since $\lambda{}\ge{}1$ for a G.2 law, $o_\nu{}\ge{}t$. Coordinatewise domination by the unrounded sum, Minkowski, and G.2's uniform per-index bound give $\|o\|_*\le{}\sqrt{C_1}a_1t$. This justifies discarding outcomes failing the good-outcome predicate. A tuple satisfying the predicate need not decode to any choice. The formal sum ranges over the bounded product of outcome slots, with impossible contributions summing to zero. Its cap is established before the move. ## What really decays

Write $V_j$ for the stage's vertex count, $Q_j$ for the quadratic edge-load norm, and $N_i$ for its component of edge type $i$. In a stage choose a least component so that $N_i\le{}Q_j/\sqrt h$. The sum over each fiber bounds the new middle-edge load of that type. Only this least-loaded type continues across stages. The outer segments inside cosets are replaced by laws with $a_1 t_\omega{}\le{}\|n_\omega{}\|+a_1$. The additive term comes from rounding the number of layers. In the formal estimate the square sum of outer loads is bounded using eight times the selected-type energy, and the middle part uses one times that energy. Minkowski gives
$$
 Q_{j+1}\le{}\sqrt{1+8C_1}\,N_i+dV_{j+1}
 \le Q_j/2+dV_{j+1},\qquad d=\sqrt{C_1}a_1/b.
$$
The manuscript's sharper calculation uses four in place of eight in the first factor. Equation (G.1) already requires $1+8C_1\le{}h/4$, so the formal proof can pay the larger constant. The middle-fiber, endpoint-segment and law-capacity conditions are actual hypotheses of the one-stage estimate. The move plan supplies them in its separate caller. **Independent derivation.** With $V_j=bV_{j+1}$ and $b\ge{}4$,
$$
 Q_{j+1}+dV_{j+1}\le{}Q_j/2+2dV_{j+1}\le{}(Q_j+dV_j)/2.
$$
Consequently $Q_j\le{}2^{-j}(Q_0+dV_0)$. Bare load can increase from zero in the scalar recurrence. If $V_j=1,d=1,Q_0=0$ at every stage, equality gives $Q_5=31/16$. The shrinkage assumption is doing quantitative work. This example probes a weakening of the recurrence and is separate from the actual tower. At a vertex, Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the upward load by $\sqrt{2h/V_j}\,Q_j$. Substituting the bound above gives a factor $(\sqrt b/2)^j/\sqrt{V_0}$. The maximum can be taken at the last stage since $b\ge{}4$. Its vertex count is bounded in terms of the fixed tower, $V_0=\Theta{}(M)$, and $2^{-J}=O(M^{-\gamma{}})$ for $\gamma{}=1/\log_2 b$. The start routes contribute at most a polylogarithmic factor times $N+M^2$ per edge. Thus one node, including its bounded number of children, receives at most $\Lambda{}M^{1-\gamma{}}(N+M^2)$ nonexceptional inter-node IDs. Summing the incident upward bounds stagewise instead gives at most $\Lambda{}M(N+M^2)$ globally, using geometric summability of $Q_j$. A bound on the global sum alone cannot supply the extra gain $M^{-\gamma{}}$ at a single cut. ## Why the exceptional packets can be charged separately

For each move/channel the formal exceptional set names its last ID $k=o_\nu{}$, including relay channels in this implementation. Its cardinality is at most the number of these channels. The proof bounds that number from the tower and finite law data, yielding $M^C$ independently of starting load $N$ and packet width $\ell{}$. Earlier IDs $k<o_\nu{}$ carry the live load. For valid initial counts and good outcomes, every current nonexceptional inter-node ID has reached a node-parent link. Initial self-loops in the formal extension remain local. The value in H.2 uses the full current cut set, so an exceptional packet crossing a partition must still be glued.
