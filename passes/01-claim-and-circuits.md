# Pass 1 · Claim and circuit coverage

Recorded on November 24, 2026 from Claude (Anthropic), *VP ≠ VNP Over Every Field*, 70-page PDF, SHA-256 `882f5eefb1e067cc8c99ddfe660c10d1d67f9fcee7b69364bd0bab06c0c1f97c`. References below use printed pages. This is the opening record of the external-paper review.

| ID | Manuscript reference | Finding and evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| P1.1 | §1.1, Theorem 1.1, pp. 1–2 | **Source statement.** There is an absolute positive c. For each characteristic p there is a threshold m₀(p) uniform over fields of characteristic p. At every m at least that threshold the claimed gate lower bound is strictly greater than m raised to c(log₂ m)^(1/3). The Hamiltonian cycle family is the witness in every characteristic. The permanent is a second witness outside characteristic two. |
| P1.2 | §1.3, pp. 3–4. Main statement printed on pp. 11–12 | **Statement correspondence checked.** The size counts all entries including inputs and constants. The target must occur in the list of formal polynomials. Gates may add or multiply any two earlier entries, including an entry with itself. A field element may enter as a constant. This permits shared gates, cancellation, unrestricted depth and a different circuit at each size. |
| P1.3 | §2.1, Theorem 2.2, p. 6. Appendix D, Theorem D.2, pp. 30–31 | **Dependency traced.** At least one geometric hypothesis holds for the coefficient field according to the theorem. Exhaustion suffices even if the cases overlap. The geometric proof itself was outside the opening pass. |
| P1.4 | Lemmas E.3–E.4, p. 33. Proposition E.10 and Theorem E.11, pp. 36–37 | **Circuit entry identified.** Affine substitution transfers an arbitrary small target circuit to a circuit for a complete-graph polynomial. Extracting components of degree one in the chosen blocks groups terms by original gate and balanced subset. Each subset has at most the circuit size in products. The sum can involve many different partitions. |
| P1.5 | §2.4, pp. 9–10. Lemmas K.2–K.3, pp. 56–57. Theorems K.6–K.7, pp. 58–59 | **Circuit entry identified.** In the second case the intermediate polynomial encodes the weighted runs of a universal program. An arbitrary circuit gives a nondeterministic evaluator with named circuit-dependent constants. Its value sums run weights. Proving that the resulting accelerator cannot exist is a separate obligation. |
| P1.6 | Lemma M.1, p. 60. Proposition M.2 and Theorem M.3, p. 61. Corollary M.4, p. 62 | **Field transfer traced.** Specialization preserves circuit size over the algebraic closure of the prime field. The Hamiltonian-cycle substitution works over every characteristic. Membership of that family in VNP then yields the stated separation if the lower bound holds. |

**Disposition at the end of pass 1.** The paper identifies circuit-wide arguments in both branches. This pass established their location and scope. It did not establish the separation independently. The next passes examine the varying-partition estimate and the weighted-program model. Theorem 1.4 treats border complexity separately.
