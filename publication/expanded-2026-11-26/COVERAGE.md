## Claim-by-claim result

`coverage-delta.csv` preserves every historical claim ID and records its baseline section and source-line span, expansion section and editorial delta. Appendix C supplies one corresponding dossier per entry, with role, statement, historical finding, evidence boundary, limitation, explanatory interface and source anchors. Historical limitations are retained as historical statements; a new prose explanation does not silently become a new formal receipt.

Section 12 treats N.1–N.5 separately without changing the historical count of 63: finite scalar truncation, simultaneous specialization, Laurent coefficient-extraction costs, round rescaling, exponent growth, a finite coefficient table and the strict modular-counter boundary. ## Delta and residual limits

The resulting manuscript has 76 pages: 34 more than the recovered draft. The increase combines deeper proof mechanisms and hypotheses with a 63-entry evidence appendix; page count itself is not a validation criterion. The original Edition 12 remains 66 pages and is not overwritten.

The conclusions remain conditional on the encoded definitions and recorded foundational axioms. No new complete Lean/Comparator run was performed in this publication session. The full implementation of the geometric limits, tower, weighted interpreter and border extractor is still supported by the publisher formalization and archived checks, not by a new independent implementation here. Outside-operator proof reproduction and independent human statement-level review remain pending. The manuscript is ready for those review steps, not represented as having completed them.
