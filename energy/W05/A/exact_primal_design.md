# Supplementary exact-primal audit

The registered primary uses a numerical primal validator and an independently evaluated rational lower bound in the deadline. A stronger claim requires a full feasible point in the same exact stored-coefficient model. This exploratory test was designed after freezing the online panel and is reported separately; its costs are completely offline. Scope is all 20 frozen 4×8 cases. For repeat zero of the LP-only arm, recover charge/discharge signs only when its raw LP point passes the original physical tolerance. Solve that fixed-mode LP to obtain a simplex basis. Set nonbasic columns and nonbasic row activities to their exact rational bounds and solve the remaining square active equations over rationals. Check the resulting candidate against all original unrestricted constraints, including original binary variables. Re-evaluate the certified unrestricted LP lower bound independently. A one-MW perturbation to grid import must fail the exact equality check. Return each witness, each refusal and any basis reconstruction failure. This audit does not change any timed action, prove an exact result on the larger cells, or address uncertainty in physical coefficients.
