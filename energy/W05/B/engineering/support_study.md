# Additional support-function test

The loose primary box bounds motivated a deterministic following question. How much does tightening each affine distortion bound on the same 25 originally certified input polytopes change the result? Keep the time-averaging map, true constraints and given anchor fixed. For each distinct nontrivial coefficient v of c(P−I) and A(P−I), solve min −vx over the full fixed-mode polytope. Use its independently rationally validated dual lower bound, negated, as an upper bound on the distortion. Take the smaller of the old box support and the nonnegative clipped new bound. A failed support solve keeps the old bound and is counted. Re-solve the reduced LP and apply the same segment reconstruction. Report cost, gap, certificate threshold and total support time against the inexpensive original full LP. The comparison is post hoc and can require many optimization calls, so it is a test of bound quality and cost rather than a proposed fast path. Initial seven failures remain in the primary denominator.
