# Prospective numerical certificate study

Eight fresh seed blocks, four fixed-mode three-device eight-hour cases per block. Time averaging pairs of consecutive hours within the same device and fixed mode reduces 24 variables to 12. It never ties battery direction binaries. Original device time constraints are checked after the lift. Main question: can computable projection and lift bounds certify small regret for all cases at a practical tolerance of 0.1% of max(1, absolute full optimal dispatch objective)? Report count meeting this tolerance using alpha+beta+epsilon and using the direct full bound. Evaluate degenerate anchor return, measured affine cost, Lipschitz vs exact cost, achieved regret, timing and fixed-mode repair with matched L1 effort. Separate candidate-specific from uniform beta. Synthetic baseline provides known anchor, whose construction/validation time is counted; acquiring an operational anchor is outside scope. All engineering constants, costs and times are synthetic. Flat-interval charges or discharge magnitudes share a sign and constant device efficiency. Projection preserves terminal equalities. For every inequality precompute upper distortion by the support of the original nameplate box; independently check feasibility and all inequalities on exact rationals. Failure of a sufficient bound is reported. LP objectives omit common baseline import cost. Terminal interventions +1,-1,+7,-7 MWh are independent model changes checked against every original nonterminal row. Report exact one-slot witnesses and multi-slot fixed-mode repair LP with minimum L1 movement, including both single-slot failure and solver-reported infeasibility. These are constructed finite cases rather than measured plants or positive-frequency estimates. No post-hoc changes to holdout selection.
