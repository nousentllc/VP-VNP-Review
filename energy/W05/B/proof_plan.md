# W05-B proof and falsification plan

This proof plan was written before the new engineering panel was inspected. Check the scalar row formula and minimum global fraction, including empty and zero-slack cases. Instantiate affine equalities, inequalities and objective on an actual convex slice. Establish an anchor displacement and objective-loss bound, a separately computable projection majorant over a box, a checked right-inverse repair followed by rechecking candidate eligibility, and a directional capacity certificate for a fixed-mode energy correction. Connect the resulting candidate lift and projection conditions to the unchanged W04 regret theorem.

Controls will include an infeasible equality after mixing, an infeasible candidate with a feasible boundary anchor, a midpoint leaving an integral or nonconvex set, infeasible multi-slot and one-slot repair claims, and an inner-model dual masquerading as a full lower bound. Use exact arithmetic independent of the Lean proof. Check sharpness or a sharper row-wise uniform envelope as a separately identified mathematical extension. Audit source and dependencies before attaching engineering significance. Finite calculations and mathematical proofs have different scopes.
