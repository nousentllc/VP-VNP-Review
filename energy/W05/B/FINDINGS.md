# W05-B · Computable loss under a checked reconstruction

The feasible-anchor proposition in the v0.4 subsection labelled `sec:repair` survives with its stated convexity, feasibility and objective assumptions. The selected fraction is the **smallest feasible fraction along that particular segment**. It can still yield a costly schedule even arbitrarily near the feasible region. The strongest practical certificate here is often a checked candidate objective and an independent lower bound. The formal projection and lift bounds remain useful when the full optimal value is unavailable.

## Feasibility and minimum movement along the segment

Let B be a convex base in a real normed vector space, x̃ and a belong to B, both obey every equality, and a satisfy every inequality. Index the finitely many inequalities by i. Write the signed affine residual as uᵢ = Aᵢx̃ − bᵢ and anchor slack as sᵢ = bᵢ − Aᵢa. Define rᵢ = uᵢ/(uᵢ+sᵢ) if uᵢ>0, and rᵢ=0 otherwise. Then λ = max({0} ∪ {rᵢ}). Empty rows give λ=0. Violated rows have a positive denominator. For any t∈[0,1], the residual at x(t)=(1−t)x̃+ta is (1−t)uᵢ−tsᵢ. A previously satisfied row stays feasible. For a violated row the residual is nonpositive exactly when t≥rᵢ. Thus every inequality holds exactly when λ≤t. Convexity and the two feasible equality endpoints give x(t)∈B and Ex(t)=d. A violated row with sᵢ=0 forces λ=1. This conclusion also covers simultaneous active constraints and repeated rows.

For affine J, its signed change is λ[J(a)−J(x̃)]. For an objective that is L-Lipschitz **on B** and ||a−x̃||≤D, the positive part of this change is at most L D λ. Under a uniform cap uᵢ≤δ̄ on violated rows and sᵢ≥σ>0 on those same rows, λ≤δ̄/(δ̄+σ). Inexact surrogates require another term. If J(x̃)−K(y)≤τ, a candidate-specific nonnegative β for affine cost is max(0,τ+λ[J(a)−J(x̃)]). A Lipschitz alternative is max(0,τ)+L D λ. Using only the change along the segment would omit the objective representation error. An exact witness with J(x̃)=J(x⁺)=7 and K(y)=2 shows that a zero movement cost can require β=5.

The numerical illustration from v0.4 reproduces in rational arithmetic. The input (3,1) and anchor (2,2) both sum to 4. Under x≤5/2 and y≤7/2 the minimum anchor fraction is 1/2 and the point (5/2,3/2) increases J=−x by 1/2. A generic Euclidean nearest-point or fixed-mode cost-minimizing repair is a separate map. The minimum t follows from restricting movement to one direction.

## Uniform α and β for an explicitly defined reduction

For full feasible type X and reduced feasible type Y, first establish that p(x)∈Y for **every** full x. Suppose the representation error is K(p(x))−J(x)=c₀+Σqⱼzⱼ(x), where lⱼ≤zⱼ(x)≤hⱼ. Then α=max(0,c₀+Σ max(qⱼlⱼ,qⱼhⱼ)) supplies a valid uniform cap. Every term is a box support. Independent sign choices attain the sum on the unconstrained box. Equalities and couplings can make it loose on X. For a genuine outer relaxation with cost-preserving embedding one may take α=0. An unrelated surrogate or a lossy restricted projection does not inherit α=0.

In the fixed-mode pair-averaging construction, p(z)=Pz, J(z)=cᵀz+c₀, K(y)=cᵀy+c₀. The signed gap for each full z is cᵀ(P−I)z. The same box calculation applied to each row of A(P−I) gives a nonnegative enlargement hᵢ making AᵢPz≤bᵢ+hᵢ. Exact membership in the reduced domain additionally requires verifying its linear image, box bounds and all required equalities. For terminal equalities E, verify E(P−I)z=0 for all eligible z, for example through E(P−I)=0. Pair averaging unequal coefficients in an energy equality can change terminal SOC. With z=(2,0), Pz=(1,1) and energy coefficients (1,1/2), the deficit is 1/2. Device-specific limits also matter when hard boxes differ across the paired coordinates.

For any eligible reduced raw point with signed residuals uᵢ≤hᵢ, one feasible fixed anchor provides the global bound λ≤maxᵢ r(hᵢ,sᵢ) over that eligible domain. This row-wise envelope also works for boundary anchors. It can be sharper than combining the largest violation of one row with the smallest margin of another. If Dⱼ bounds max(0,J(a)−J(x̃)) across the reduced domain, and J(x̃)−K(y)≤τ uniformly, then β=max(0,τ)+Dⱼ maxᵢ r(hᵢ,sᵢ) works for affine cost. One computable Dⱼ is the anchor cost minus a full-box lower support bound on raw cost, floored at zero. For an ε-suboptimal reduced return y, both a uniform full-to-reduced α and a checked feasible lift x⁺ yield J(x⁺)−inf J≤α+β+ε. The candidate version needs the lift at the returned y. Infima over nonempty cost ranges bounded below are the W04 convention. A fixed-mode optimality comparison concerns that slice; an unrestricted MIP comparison requires a true full-model lower bound or a uniform construction over its entire disjunction.

Computationally, with both row vectors already evaluated, the fraction takes one pass over the rows. Computing residuals, exact cardinality-dependent box products, independent validation, obtaining a feasible anchor, checking the reduced gap and the time to generate a useful point each have a cost. The numerical track measures a specified implementation. Exact proofs supply no bound on those wall times.

## Equality correction and its budget

For a tentative w with residual r=d−Ew choose a checked mapping R satisfying ERr=r. Then w′=w+Rr satisfies the full equality system. A global right inverse satisfies ER=I. Full row rank is sufficient in finite dimensions, with R=Eᵀ(EEᵀ)⁻¹ as one possible construction. A verified nonsingular column minor supplies another, usually with different inequality effects. With dependent rows one can select independent rows, verify target consistency and test all original equalities, or check ERr=r just for the measured residual. The implementation should verify the identity at its stated arithmetic accuracy and charge conditioning and construction costs. Mixing the original uncorrected residual with a feasible anchor leaves residual (1−λ)(Ew−d). Consequently, exact equality after mixing requires λ=1 or an already-zero residual, allowing both.

After correction, w′ must still be eligible for the same mode and convex base. Re-evaluate every inequality. An equality correction can leave a box or open new violations. In the explicit control E=[1,0], d=1, w=(0,0), R=(1,2)ᵀ, ER=1 but w′=(1,2) violates y≤1. A two-stage affine accounting uses J(w′)−J(w)=cᵀRr, followed by λ[J(a)−J(w′)]. If using a Lipschitz envelope, its domain must cover each evaluated pair. Distinct binary schedules generally have fractional mixtures. A unit-voltage equality admits scalar voltages −1 and +1 but rejects their midpoint. Full numerical AC feasibility therefore needs separate reasoning.

## A signed storage slot with explicit coupling limits

Start from a plan in one fixed charge/discharge mode satisfying every requirement except a terminal energy mismatch. Choose one device and period, an increment or decrement in the existing allowed direction, and nonnegative magnitude q in MW. Fix the other controls. With interval duration Δt and efficiencies ηc,ηd>0, changing charge by ±q changes terminal energy by κq = ±ηcΔt q. Changing discharge by ±q gives κq = ∓Δt q/ηd. A terminal equality E*=e+κq therefore fixes q=(E*−e)/κ, provided κ≠0 and q≥0. For a terminal tolerance band outside the current e, its nearest eligible edge gives the least magnitude along this direction. Within the band q=0 suffices.

State balances for all later times shift the selected device's SOC by exactly κq, assuming fixed efficiencies, no leakage and no subsequent control changes. Grid import at the selected period shifts by +Δcharge−Δdischarge in MW. The entering and leaving ramp differences shift by equal magnitudes with opposite signs. At the first period the entering ramp uses the known pre-horizon import; at the end include a post-horizon ramp whenever the original model has one. Power boxes, directional nonnegativity, availability, reserves/headroom, SOC upper and lower rows for the entire suffix, grid import/export, both incident ramp rows, and all other contractual coupling rows belong in the check. Each inequality has an original nonnegative slack sᵢ and signed increase uᵢq. All rows hold exactly iff q≤sᵢ/uᵢ for every uᵢ>0. Nonpositive slopes consume no slack for q≥0. Other equality rows must have zero directional coefficient, or be tested as two opposite inequalities with their actual residuals. This yields a computable certificate for one direction. Failure for every tried direction does not imply infeasibility of multi-slot or unrestricted repair. Two charge slots each with 1 MWh headroom can jointly repair a deficit of 2 MWh even though neither alone can.

For an affine energy tariff and linear wear, the signed incremental cost is the original per-slot cost gradient times q. A positive charging increment q with marginal import price p and throughput charge k costs (p+k)Δt q if it remains in the specified tariff segment. A discharging increment reduces imports while potentially increasing wear. Crossed tariff segments, maximum-demand charges or nonlinear wear require evaluating the actual objective. From a feasible anchor in the same slice, ordinary fixed-mode repair LP can select many coordinated slots or repair to lower objective. Finding no cheap one-slot certificate establishes only a limitation of the chosen direction set.

## Verification scope

The source in formal/AIPC.lean is byte-matched to W04. New source modules contain theorem statements, proofs and constructive counterexamples. The statement and transitive-axiom inventory is in results/lean/Inventory.log. The direct numeric controls in exact_controls.py evaluate exact rational schedules and polytope rows separately from the Lean proofs. The grid tested 3,616 systems with up to three rows, including the empty set, and 21,497 feasibility comparisons. Of these systems, 820 already satisfied all rows and 1,236 forced the boundary anchor. In 68 finite systems the realized row-specific fraction was strictly less than the coarse positive-margin upper bound. The box support was attained in all 27 test objectives across 729 coordinate points. For 960 signed single-slot queries over 32 fixed-mode test trajectories, direct recomputation of all constraints passed 574 and rejected 386. These are constructed controls, with no sampling interpretation.

The Lean theorems model real arithmetic, linear/affine maps, finite row lists and a checked convex slice. The engineering matrix constructor, exact Python code, floating solver, real actuator and measurements are outside their trust boundary. The heterogeneous fixed-mode energy and time-coupling consequences are formally decomposed into the suffix sum, ramp difference and signed row-capacity theorems. Mapping a particular grid model into those rows remains a model-generation obligation. Sources, fingerprints, failed attempts, proof commands and prior art appear in README.md, LITERATURE.md, proof_lock.json and results/. The separately labelled two-anchor construction and boundary discontinuity are in EXPLORATORY.md.
