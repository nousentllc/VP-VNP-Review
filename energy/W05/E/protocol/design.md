The primary unit is a latent synthetic day. Use W04 for development, then evaluate seeds 15101–15160 from the original generator under ordinary, biased-forecast and outage/reserve scenarios. Independently implement a second generator and reserve seeds 22501–22524 for a distinct out-of-generator sensitivity. Do not pool the panels. In the alternative process a 7–10 hour elevated workload and a second short pulse interact with persistent AR(0.78) load errors and correlated lead errors. Outage timing varies independently of the peak and precedes the final recharge ramp. The three variants of a seed share realized load. No measured operational trace is assumed.

All policies see current truth, eligible issued forecast prefixes, and the published reserve/floor calendar for that prefix. The explicit availability forecast reveals only the present and next hour. The W04 reserve calendar may signal the peak/outage window earlier. Protected facility load 24 MW, grid cap 100 MW, import ramp 23 MW/h, battery 35 MW/180 MWh, charge/discharge efficiencies 0.94/0.93, opening/closing floor 95 MWh. ## Frozen policies and training

Three rule choices at H=3,6,12 are W04's original target, doubled future deficit with W04 baseline inventory, and filling to nameplate whenever free import and storage capacity permit. Each serves the current deficit as aggressively as physical constraints permit. Select lowest mean flexible unserved MWh/day on the 24 W04 latent days averaged equally over the three regimes at each horizon, ties in that order. Include legacy unscaled rules at H6 and H12 separately. The rolling LP baseline minimizes total forecast shedding, then battery throughput, then the current shed, charge and discharge in that order. The W04 target from its recorded eight-day development calibration inserts a soft terminal-shortfall objective immediately after shedding. The W05 learned target uses the 90th percentile of the next up-to-eight hours' peak cumulative net SOC draw among 48 nearest neighbors in (clock hour/3, end load/12 MW). Fit from 24 W04 ordinary days only, use the issued terminal forecast in place of the unavailable realized load, cap by nameplate and remove the extra at the day boundary. All three rolling policies use the same matrix, hard bounds and solver. Reuse one HiGHS simplex model across lexicographic objectives inside each decision with each previous optimum constrained. Verify the original W04 implementation at 27 representative W04 states. First action is lexicographically unique to the numerical tolerance. This tests policy design, without testing faster solvers.

H12-information control nested6 explicitly ignores forecast entries beyond H6 and applies learned6. Another control nestedrule6 discards the same tail and applies the chosen H6 rule. With both H6 and H12 candidate solves paid for, adaptive selects H6 only when 7–12h mean issued load is more than 4 MW below the mean six-hour head. Fixed12 pays for both and always takes H12. ## Endpoints and falsification

Freeze exactly two confirmatory contrasts on the 60 first-generator days. Learned H6 minus the best development-selected H6 rule, and learned H12 minus the lowest-shedding W04-development H12-information comparator among rule12, nestedrule6, none12, w0412, nested6 and adaptive. Report which comparator won selection, every component score, and effects versus all alternatives. Nested policies are eligible for the H12 comparator. Strict practical improvement requires an upper one-sided 97.5% day-cluster bootstrap bound below −0.1 MWh/day for unserved energy. Use 10,000 paired percentile bootstrap samples, seed 509051, separately within panel. Bonferroni allocates 0.025 per confirmatory contrast in a one-sided family of two. Each seed's primary service value is its equally weighted three-regime average. Regime-specific, other horizons and the alternative generator are descriptive. A selected-family claim also requires zero hard physical violations, equal outage reservation coverage, no missing case in that contrast, upper 97.5% bound no greater than +0.005 in fallback fraction of hours, and upper 97.5% bound no greater than +5 MWh/day in excess upper-tail loss (mean of the worst ceil(10% × sample-size) seed-average daily shed values in each arm). Bootstrap gates describe this generator and can be degenerate for rare failures. Report exact independent-day zero-failure upper limits separately. Count missing actions and failed solves; any missing trajectory blocks that contrast.

Report explicit H12-to-H6 policy comparison and H12 forecast vs flat-tail realized service and forecast MAE/RMSE by common targets, as well as adaptive vs dual-solve fixed. Faster decisions, superior forecasts, and universally more useful information each require separate evidence. Physical actions require one precomputed and validated common one-step fallback per state. Charge candidate solve, predicate evaluation and check on a one-second wall gate; record fallback-ready, candidate-ready, actual selected action and full overall runtime. The synchronous implementation has no real-time watchdog or measured actuation. Compare each trajectory on identical inputs, policies and original observed timeout signals. Log new timings and never credit that controlled-clock replay as another independent day or a second live timing panel.
