# AC-OPF results

The prespecified criterion failed against every comparator. On 24, 73, 200 and 500 buses, the guided/full matched median paired wall ratios were 0.978, 0.995, 0.988 and 1.004. Five repeats qualified per size. The registered gain was 10%. All five methods returned invalid solutions on all five 1,888-bus trials at the registered 12-second gate. No matched timing on that case qualifies. See analysis/matched.csv and analysis/trials.csv for all individual ratios and absolute costs. All 100 admissible points also passed the independent implementation and its corruption controls.

The guided graph policy eliminates paired real and reactive generator coordinates where a frozen curvature and fill heuristic allows it. This is a different mechanism from tying decisions across devices. The nonlinear model retains native pivot generator limits after substitution. The fitted solver optimizes locally. The common absolute angle boxes are redundant on three cases by exact directed-path bounds. Distances from the reference bus leave 88 and 409 boxes unresolved on the other two, respectively. The common matched starts agree in original variables, although the internal bound push and solved KKT systems can differ. On the largest network the start violates a generator range by as much as approximately 8,773 in MW or MVAr.

## Supplemental 60-second diagnostic

The standard IPOPT report showed zero numerical-factorization time for its MUMPS build; these zeros were considered unavailable, motivating a separate instrumented execution of all 25 case-policy cells. Backend operation, elapsed factor time, and entry counters were extracted from per-process native logs. Counts of completed reports need not be equal when iterations are interrupted or a timer is not emitted. Identical objective, structure and start do not guarantee deterministic wall performance. This is one repetition per cell with increased budget and verbosity, separate from the five-repeat primary experiment.

Twenty-three of 25 instrumented cells yielded independently valid results within 60 seconds. On the 1,888-bus case, conventional full, matched full and degree-based elimination qualified in 33.27, 23.04 and 24.45 seconds. All-pivot and guided were physically invalid at about 62 seconds. Their peak reported factor entry count was 20,476,919 each, against 5,657,717 for matched full and 1,433,580 for the degree policy. MUMPS reported cumulative completed-factor wall times of 43.49 seconds for guided, 9.96 for matched full and 9.78 for degree. Degree removes 278 pivots, only two fewer than all/guided, while changing fill and solver progress. This one intervention does not isolate a causal role for particular buses or a single ordering.
