# Independent W05-C formulation and cutoff review

Four arms use a common physical initial point. The implemented matched full model distributes the AC net residual at every generator bus over the online units in proportion to their individual P/Q ranges (equally when the range sum is zero). Consequently each of those bus balances holds at the initial point, up to floating arithmetic. Any chosen pivot at such a bus can be recovered by subtracting all other injections. The conventional full DC reference is a separate deliberately different start. The matched tuple may violate original P/Q boxes and non-generator balance. IPOPT can push the full and reduced starts differently inside its bounds.

check_initials.py independently parses the text MATPOWER tables, removes offline assets, rebuilds sparse DC susceptances, and computes end flows from transformer-adjusted internal current, line charging and complex voltage. It derives the common start, checks each stored first-repeat initial tuple and generator-box diagnostic, reconstructs the guided, degree and all-pivot score/sets, and verifies original dimensions. A separate componentwise calculation on every terminal vector tests branch ends, nodal P/Q, rated powers, angles and voltage, reference angle, generator boxes, finite values, cost and the 12-second cutoff. The original online check is a separate complex sparse-admittance implementation. The newly computed start shares the native merit-order tie convention and the original equations.

All 25 first-repeat tuples passed. Across the three reduced arms and five cases the maximum coordinate discrepancy against the corresponding saved full-matched point was 2.273e-10 (largest component in generated power). Rebuilding from raw data independently gave less than 2.15e-10 MW/MVAr discrepancy per generator output and less than 8e-14 radians per angle. Original generator range violations before the optimizer bound push reach 8,773 MW or MVAr on 1,888 RTE. Matching the same large mismatch removes that particular confound between arms, while leaving all arms exposed to poor initialization.

All 125 saved final vectors and all 25 first-repeat start diagnostics were present. Exactly 100 terminal vectors pass physical constraints, all within 12 seconds, with zero disagreements in eligibility or validity. The 25 remaining candidates are from 1,888 RTE and fail the physical check. Every one-MW generator-injection corruption on a valid returned point was rejected. Solver cutoff scores use the independent validation timestamp and do not depend on the status name. Worker imports and post-clock KKT/diagnostic construction are excluded. The current real-time control is a scoring gate; the source sets the internal solver allowance before constructing IPOPT and can return well after 12 seconds.

On each selected bus, substituting the pivot is algebraically exact for the implemented static model because the two balances have unit coefficients in individual P,Q and all cost and generator-box uses are updated. Guided chooses zero curvature before comparing ranges, then charges a clique-size penalty on a curved pivot and counts missing neighbor-to-neighbor edges. This score is a local heuristic. It ignores numerical scaling, active constraints, the KKT elimination ordering and possible duplicate fill edges. The 1,888-bus all and guided arms are algebraically identical. Every generation cost there is linear, both choose exactly the same 280 pivots, and any numerical-clock difference there cannot establish an advantage from structural selection. Other cases provide real treatment variation. The 500-bus guided arm still selects 60 curved pivots.

## Additional exact angle-box test

For an active branch impose lower ≤ θ_i−θ_j ≤ upper. An arc from j to i of weight upper gives an upper bound on θ_i−θ_j. The reverse arc has weight −lower. Summing arcs along a path from the zero reference gives an upper bound for an absolute angle, and a return path gives the corresponding lower bound. All extracted weights here are nonnegative, so rational Dijkstra computes the strongest directed path bounds. The exact decimal degree values come from the original active rows. No AC solution or solver tolerance enters this argument. When both limits lie within ±360 degrees at every bus, the added angle boxes are redundant globally on the implemented branch constraints. The implication was verified for 24, 73, and 200 buses. The source path test alone cannot resolve all buses on 500 and 1,888. A loose bound is not an example of an excluded AC-feasible solution.
