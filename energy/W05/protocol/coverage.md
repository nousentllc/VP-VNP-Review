Coverage limits apply across tracks. B and E lead the research decisions. Independent workstreams were prepared and run on the same workstation with controlled solver thread counts. There was no reserved timing core or second machine. Paired repeats and same-machine replays therefore have narrower scope than independent hardware replication. Public benchmarks and prior authors’ theorems are independent intellectual sources; W05 constructions, replications, proof-to-model mappings and interpretations originate in this project. Systemwide savings and site operating value remain unmeasured.
