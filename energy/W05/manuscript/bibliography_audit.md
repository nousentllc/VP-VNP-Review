# Bibliography identity audit

On 24 November 2026 each of the 46 bibliography items was matched in source order to the accompanying CSV. The structural check independently found 46 distinct cited keys and resolved every key. This audit compares the bibliography with author, repository, publisher and agency records. The mathematical validity of external articles and the data behind third-party estimates were outside this identity check.

All 36 public entries identify an original publication, project record or author audit. Thirty received a direct identity match without a substantive bibliographic qualification. Six carry specific notes. Nine of ten internal entries match an artifact by content, source digest or archive inspection. The tenth, the Chat v0.2 research evidence archive, is a 62,297-byte ZIP identified from the private version register by its label and file metadata. This review did not inspect its members. The CSV records each lookup route and distinguishes archived observations from directly retrieved content.
