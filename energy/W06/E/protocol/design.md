# W06-E preregistration, before holdout generation

Decision unit: one small synthetic price-taking site on each of eight independent seed families, each with a seven-day and a thirty-day trajectory (paired random seeds; horizon-specific end treatment). Development seeds 61011 and 61027, holdout seeds 69103, 69119, 69143, 69157, 69179, 69203, 69221, 69239. Every seed and both horizons are retained. Equal seed weights per horizon; no significance attributed to individual hours. W05 is a historical regression only.

Model. Hourly plant, integer SOC 0–12 MWh, permanent operating range 2–11 for upward energy and downward absorption margin, initial SOC 9, initial signed battery block 0. Charge/discharge power uses discrete signed internal MWh blocks -2 through 2 with no simultaneous operation. Charging consumes 1.1 grid MWh/block; discharge delivers 0.9/block. Maximum setpoint change 2 blocks/hour across all boundaries. Symmetric nominal action power up to two blocks; mandatory upward reserve is one block in evening and downward one at night, subject to available power; outages explicitly suspend the reserve contract. A derating can reduce power to one or zero and strand the bottom five MWh for discharge. Optional one-block one-hour upward availability contract pays its offered hourly capacity fee only with spare power and energy. Actual dispatch/activation, real prices, physical certification and settlement are outside scope. Objective is private operating cost including reserve receipts. Degradation proxy $8/MWh of AC-side throughput and an extra $6/block for the second block in an hour (rate stress). Preserve individual cost and service components.

Jobs are discrete fixed-energy batches with booked service slots two/five hours after release. Critical/high-priority batches use 0.7 MWh, low-priority batches 0.4 MWh. Obligation queues cross midnight and each job can complete only at its booked slot. The primary contract serves all protected load and booked jobs, with hard grid import 9 MW. Differentiated values in the separate economic-tradeoff arm are mandatory high-priority jobs and $18 per missed low-priority job. This stylized workload is independent of the battery state and contains no intra-window rescheduling. Project final outstanding jobs by closing admissions that would extend beyond the horizon; report cross-midnight obligations and energy. Hard minimum served load and reserve margins are independently checked under each trajectory. Throughput efficiency and credits have common boundaries and units.

Issued information. H12 snapshots contain metered current price, availability, demand, active queues, and deterministic schedules, with a damped-persistence price forecast anchored to the time-of-day mean. Future bookings known only after release; conditional mean forecasts apply to unannounced jobs. Future outages are uncertain, except for a publicly clear last 16 hours enabling guaranteed terminal reachability. All online controllers see the same issued array; H6 explicitly ignores the final six. Construct safety using a public final recovery window, with one-block robust charge/discharge and idle transitions, and a fixed final SOC of 9 on both sides for the primary comparison. For the separate floor contract use terminal credit $50/MWh and the same SOC floor of 2. Record credits at $0/$50/$100 for sensitivity. Full information is reserved for offline references.

Offline first. Reconstruct W05 exact greedy actions and replay both economic arms, then certify the exact retrospective optimum on the finite plant by integer dynamic programming with complete stage potentials and independent edge checks. Evaluate opportunity relative to a deployable price-aware rule. Separate stock-only effect by comparing optimally dispatched matched fill-end stock with a floor on exactly the same tariff, service and terminal credit; run isolated W05-like flat tariff at $50, $10/AC MWh throughput, no second-block penalty, no ancillary bid, $50 terminal credit. Split comparison is an artificial independent-day reset on the same realized hour inputs (includes the carried jobs at their actual service slots), never an operating comparator. Include variable tariff, fixed hourly service, credit and optional low-service tradeoff in separate records.

Comparator roster: W05-style fill tracks a 4.0 MW nominal import threshold with available surplus; price-aware rule uses a wear-adjusted reference price and returns to the common terminal target; rolling no-tail optimization H12 and H6 exact discrete shortest-path/flow LP solve over common issued information. Price rule selects its reference from 55, 70, 85 dollars/MWh on the two seven-day development paths by mean cost, ties in list order. A conditional terminal-value controller may enter only if mean physical ceiling against the price rule exceeds $15/site-day on a horizon. No new controller training before that gate. Its terminal credit is selected from $45, $65, $85 per MWh on the same two disjoint development paths and used only on eligible horizons. Wall deadline 1 second per hour including safe action, policy plan, validation and fallback. In cases of violation substitute the same admissible price-rule action. No operational deadline guarantee is inferred. Negative wins and all clocks remain.

Inferential gates: compare the gated policy against both price rule and H12 no-tail on 7 and 30 days, one-sided exact seed sign tests for daily (reference cost minus candidate cost)/days above $15. Holm step-down over four tests at 5%; ineligible hypotheses contribute p=1. Report distribution-free conservative two-sided median intervals [sample minimum, maximum] (99.22% per contrast at n=8 for independent continuous seed-level differences, conservative with ties). Report descriptive paired means and extremes separately. Any improvement requires equal terminal SOC, no protected/priority/deadline/mandatory-reserve violations, unchanged primary jobs, and no empirical trajectory with worse reliability. Unknown integration costs yield at most a gross/break-even budget, no positive net implementation claim. Monetary model excludes wholesale-system, facility investment and emissions effects. Multiple tests are exploratory for external validity. No emissions or accreditation claims.

Serialize actual paths, issued forecasts, per-hour actions and clocks, offline Bellman witnesses, release checks, causal fallback and failures. Verify reference/candidate trajectories independently. Time historical regression, generation, model construction, certificate, comparator/replay and checking. Wall clock includes host contention. Direct objective proof applies only to the encoded discrete one-hour plant.
