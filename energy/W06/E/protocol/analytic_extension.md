# Exploratory analytic bound, after inspecting the splice LP ceilings

All 30-day matched-stock ceilings were tiny. Test whether a simple energy-balance argument can certify matched-stock saturation and bound the entire remaining inventory effect. For nonnegative ks=(p+w)/eta_c-v and kd=(p+w)*draw/eta_c+w-p, fix hourly service and write cost as constant + ks*(end-initial) + kd*sum(discharge). Compute mandatory discharge below the served-load envelope from a cap, upward grid ramp, charge power, and optional backward ramp tightening. With cumulative excess discharge e, fill's equal-closing-stock opportunity ceiling is kd*e and relaxed-closing-stock ceiling is ks*(fill_end-floor)+kd*e. Treat any nonzero e as unresolved. Check all 252 originals and all 12 preselected splices exactly. This calculation uses W05's literal binary64 efficiencies as rational coefficients. A fixed-stock path with extra charge/discharge or heterogeneous prices can defeat the saturation premise.
