# Original-scale W05 continuity stress test, supplementary

The new synthetic finite-state site has 12 MWh and its dollars cannot be scaled into the earlier 180 MWh W05 site. An explicit historical bridge therefore applies the archived W05 plant (no changed units, tariff, efficiencies, grid ramp, energy floor or reserve schedule) to splices of independent archived synthetic days. It carries both end SOC and grid import into the next day and enforces the archived floors and hourly service from that recomputed feasible fill. These artificial paths are not a sample of real chronological operations. This test is post-primary and cannot change W06 confirmation.

Use two disjoint 30-day blocks of the primary panel, sorted by seed within each of base and outage-reserve. Use the first seven paths in each 30-day block for a paired 7-day sensitivity. For the alternative base and outage-reserve panels use first seven in each of two blocks of twelve. Biased forecasts reuse physical scenarios and are excluded. Report every failure; do not substitute a tractable path. Compute exact fill costs and rational boxed weak-dual lower bounds at identical hourly service. Compare the two upper ceilings at identical closing stock and at original floor plus $50/MWh. The difference of two ceilings alone does not certify the objective difference between the true optima. A positive upper ceiling establishes only unresolved opportunity. Preserve multipliers, exact gaps, case order and grid/SOC states. No baseline resets, independent-day aggregation or real deployment inference.
