# Fixed-schedule rainflow sensitivity, exploratory

Freeze before computing the revaluation. Score every stored fixed-final-stock arm on all 16 previously analyzed 7/30-day paths, separately for the original scheduled-headroom and one-call standby contracts. Reconstruct energy cost, reserve receipts and original step wear; drop both original wear terms and substitute per-path depth wear. Do not reoptimize, reselect policies, add activation, or retune any model. Compare continuation with price and three-scenario dispatch at pack replacement prices $50, $150 and $300 per nameplate kWh. The first two prices are illustrative stress values, the last follows an older published case-study assumption. They are not current prices. The synthetic 12 MWh site has no cell chemistry or calibrated pack replacement cost.

Use Xu et al. (2017 arXiv v2, Eq. 24) Phi(d)=0.000524*d^2.03 per full cycle for its example 18650 NMC cells. Use full uninterrupted SOC from the common initial to closed final stock. Remove plateaus, count closed inner cycles as full and residual ranges as half cycles, without repeating the path periodically or clearing the stack at midnight. Use the earlier exact cost lower bound only after subtracting an upper bound on all feasible old wear and adding nonnegative new wear.
