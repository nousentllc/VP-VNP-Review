## Screening cost and direct optimization

For cost the corresponding totals were 1.962, 3.049 and 1.017 seconds. All arms rebuilt and verified the same baseline. Among 70 eligible cases, the median of three per-case service timings favored staged screening over direct DP on 38 cases and favored DP on 32. Direct DP was quicker on 46/70 cost cases. The median paired staged-minus-DP differences were −1.25 ms and +5.59 ms, respectively. Long difficult cases can reverse a majority of paired wins in the aggregate. Staged screening was quicker than native MILP on all 70 service case medians and 51/70 cost medians. ## Falsification and strengthened comparator

Eight constructed cases hit their independently known optima, including 0.75/1/2 MWh and $15/$20/$40 alternatives bracketing the targets, plus two exact-zero cases. No instance with an attainable weak target was excluded. Across the frozen comparison and controls, the native incumbent matched the independent DP optimum in all 140 eligible objective cells and eight controls. Native reported lower bounds matched within 1e−6 native units in all 148 checks. Independently recomputed LP bounds never exceeded the whole-model discrete optimum. Invalid SOC, a flipped inequality sign and a missing panel outcome were rejected. The backwards audit traversed 267,606 feasible state/action arcs. [Controls](results/controls.json) · [Replay receipt](analysis/verification.json)

After observing the primary results, we tried two eight-hour local sweeps starting from each primary witness. We fixed the original target and, for cost, every hour of the original delivered service. Each extra schedule was checked against the original constraints and independent optimum. The number of exclusions rose from 56 to 67/72 for service and 49 to 61/72 for cost. Among the 70 eligible paths, mean available improvement fell from 0.582 to 0.100 MWh/path and from $72.99 to $15.51/path. The modeled paths span either 12 or 24 hours; the figures are per modeled path. The median incremental planning/validation time was 1.82 ms for service and 0.81 ms for cost. Three negative-price/outage paths still met the service target. Seven time-of-use paths and two negative-price/outage paths still met the cost target. Post-primary reuse limits inference. ## Decisions and open obligations

Retain simple service power and energy envelopes for triage. On comparable small discretized systems, benchmark direct exact state search before adopting a full LP certificate stack. Retire the claim that lattice rounding increased exclusion coverage here. Advance only the residual outage and time-of-use schedules into richer information/plant contracts. Calibrate energy increments, degradation, controller information, reserve obligations, initial/terminal conditions and actual cost before transferring results. All-feasible perfect schedules for a single quantized storage unit are exhaustible; scaling to multiple devices, finer SOC grids or continuous inverter dynamics can change both headroom and timing. The secondary direct MILP does not emit an independently replayed branch-and-bound proof; exact optimality here comes from the integer model's independent state recurrence. Missing or physically rejected baseline fixtures never establish plant infeasibility.
