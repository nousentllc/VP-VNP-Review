# Bounded disjunctive refinement of open commitment cases

Post hoc after observing the February 24-hour LP gap in preliminary validations. Retain all primary cases and their budgets. For each final case open at 1%, test whether a small independently certified commitment tree narrows the interval. Split the leaf with smallest verified floor on its most fractional unfixed commitment indicator, then other binary indicators if necessary. Up to 31 full LP solves including root. Each child's row dual is rechecked on its branch's complete boxes. Inherit the parent floor, even if the new suggestion is unusable. Both children must be represented, including native infeasibility without a checked proof. Stop when the target is excluded or the budget is spent. This is a same-case sensitivity, with no new prevalence estimate.
