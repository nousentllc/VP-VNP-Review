The reviewer read the frozen W07-E protocol, the W06 supplementary and one-call price policies, the generator and controller, and the filed raw tables. A separate standard-library program used forward reachable-state minimization and direct integer cash accounting. It imports neither the simulation nor the original checkers. ## Decision-time information

From each of 864 observed current rows, current bookings and the public calendar, the independent program reconstructed all 8,712 future H12 rows exactly. For lead $k$, the price uses the public time-of-day level and current price residual, the next-hour power forecast persists current power for one hour outside the final public window, and later power is forecast at two. Forecast bookings use arrivals announced at or before the decision, with the declared fixed default for later unknown arrivals. The emulator never saw a future actual row. Across the four policy arms, 49,994 completed action-hours had self-consistent physical transitions, budget decrements and exact cash components. The observed nominal command was unique in each of 27,047 arm/family/budget/time/observed-state groups. The three-scenario robust arm used zero fallback actions in these recorded hours.

All dated demand and future outage support remain model assumptions. H12 rows come from a synthetic issuance rule. The exploratory calendar certificate grants tomorrow's exogenous data while withholding the request identity except through delivered calls. A product in which denied/outage requests are observable, a counterparty announces a future call, or the available forecast has different meaning requires a revised information partition.

## Original-policy coverage of the retrospective oracle

For the universal posted contract, admissibility requires recovering through hour 72 after any permitted future available power and call. Given today's revealed power, a nominal $q$ is admissible only if both the zero-call branch and every still-permitted posted call obey today's cash-independent physics and admit a safe continuation. Budget-dependent guards in the oracle therefore retain every universally safe causal path. Revealing actual exogenous inputs and calls only to the objective minimizer can make its cost lower. The load-extrema check introduces no restriction on commands over the declared input range. At the greatest permissible discharge, minimum total load 18 deciMWh exactly offsets 18 deciMWh released. At the greatest charge, maximum load 48 plus 22 equals 70 below the feeder's 90-deciMWh limit. Observed total loads covered 18–48. Bounds still depend on no optional service shedding, the original standby headroom even at exhausted budget, fixed SOC 9 closing stock, common cost, mandatory call substitution and a public outage-free final sixteen hours.

I independently reconstructed the minimum through forward label propagation over all reached SOC/ramp pairs for all 192 paths. Each integer total agreed with its archived root. From the fresh cash logs, the K=2 mean ceiling was exactly $8361/160 per site-day ($52.25625) and the single-scenario policy's mean saving against the robust reference was $-413/60 per site-day. One of 48 K=2 path-specific ceilings fell strictly below $15. The weighted causal $15 target remains unresolved. Neither the $52.26 ceiling nor its $49.50 partial-information successor demonstrates that a positive gain is achievable.

A model with reliability required only on the actually realized path permits more actions. With the same realized ramp, load, headroom, budget, costs and terminal stock, dropping universal off-path recovery lowered 135 of 192 optima. The largest difference occurred on seed 73433, K=2, standby: the universally guarded retrospective minimum was 1,659,660 cents and the path-only minimum was 1,623,060 cents, a $366 difference over three days. This counterexample identifies the scope of the stronger floor. Exogenous-risk or chance-constrained programs and programs obligated only to the four tested calendars need a separate coverage proof.

## What to carry forward

The fresh comparator preserves W06's three-scenario price architecture, shared first action, lead-scaled price perturbations, separate future planning and tail mark. W06 included an optional upward bid and an actual per-decision one-second fallback test. W07 uses a mandatory-only product, different safety masks and precomputed feedback tables without that deadline test. Describe a contract-matched adaptation of W06's architecture. Aggregate runtimes here cannot establish W06 action-deadline equivalence.

Matched completed traces serve every fixed booking by construction, with integer import and all required jobs charged at their assigned hours. Failed arms stop at the first disqualified action; the full cost and interrupted-service value of that failed trajectory are missing. The study supplies neither discretionary shedding economics nor a measured cost of lost service or proof of site reliability. Availability tariff receipts and rainflow costs are illustrative. The exogenous power guarantee and fixed endpoints are essential assumptions. All checks in this review share the case files and declared contract; they supply a second implementation on the same machine.
