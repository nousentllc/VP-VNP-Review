# W07-B primary-source comparison

| Original work | Result used and boundary |
|---|---|
| R. T. Rockafellar and R. J.-B. Wets (1991), *Scenarios and Policy Aggregation in Optimization Under Uncertainty*, Mathematics of Operations Research 16, 119–147. [Publisher and original paper](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.16.1.119) | Bundle scenarios at levels determined by the information available. The W07 infimum ordering follows by adding valid common-action conditions. W07 neither proves convergence of progressive hedging nor reports a new scenario-decomposition algorithm. |
| D. B. Brown, J. E. Smith and P. Sun (2010), *Information Relaxations and Duality in Stochastic Dynamic Programs*, Operations Research 58, 785–801, especially §2. [Authors' original paper](https://people.duke.edu/~psun/bio/opre.1090.0796v1.pdf) | Their general information relaxation and dual-feasible penalty provide weak duality and, with additional structure, strong-duality results. W07 uses the minimization form with a nonpositive expected additive penalty uniformly over all admissible policies. The debit variant subtracts an explicit worst-case expectation bound. The nonzero centering defect example illustrates the need for that bound. Tightness of the toy's chosen penalty is a finite instance. |
| D. P. Bertsekas (1971), *Control of Uncertain Systems with a Set-Membership Description of the Uncertainty*, MIT PhD dissertation. [MIT repository](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/b5a26a42-ebd8-4503-b4d7-a46c468538cc) | Worst-case target reachability under set-valued uncertainty and feedback predates this work. The W07 contribution is the precise action/call/recourse/disturbance order, checkable finite contract and formal link to a causal contingency tree. The proof requires observation of a sufficient successor state. |
| E. J. Horvitz (1987), *Reasoning about Beliefs and Actions under Computational Resource Constraints*, UAI. [Author's record and paper](https://www.erichorvitz.com/u87.htm) | Resource-limited decisions motivate charging certification, direct solution, baseline acquisition and checking. A measured upper ceiling excludes a stated gain on a specific contract; by itself it gives no distribution of the value of future compute or of engineering deployment. |
| S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe (2004), *Convex Optimization*, §§5.2.2, 5.5.1. [Authors' book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf) | Primal feasible and dual bounds yield an objective interval. The strict threshold test in W07 is a direct arithmetic consequence; it also applies to nonconvex finite models whenever a lower certificate is valid. |

These primary sources concern different technical settings. The checkable W07 artifacts instantiate established arguments. The literature comparison is bounded and supports no general priority claim. Prior internal W06 verification and the private 52-claim review are historical evidence with a shared specification.
