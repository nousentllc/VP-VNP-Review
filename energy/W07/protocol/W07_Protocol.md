# CRGC W07 — prospective protocol (v0.6)

**Purpose:** Determine the value still attainable under actual decision-time information and a defined activation/recovery contract. W06 established exact flat-tariff saturation, finite-panel opportunity exclusions and valid AC/UC ceilings; its most complex tested controller did not outperform the supplementary three-scenario policy. No W07 result is established. COA is the primary measurement framework; AIPC remains supporting assurance.

## W07-B — Mathematical foundations (critical)

Define a finite chronological scenario tree with nonnegative original weights, explicitly observed history and available issued forecasts. An admissible deterministic policy maps observed history (including endogenous state, previous command, obligations and available time) to actions. For finite policies/randomized variants specify the admissible class and any permitted random seed. Prove the ordered infima for valid nested information relaxations under one objective and identical physical/risk contract. A relaxed scenario program must include the projection of every admissible policy; do not mistake a feasible incumbent or restricted policy value for a lower bound. Show that coarsening partitions strengthens nonanticipativity, provided state/action and contract semantics match.

Develop a hierarchy: per-path perfect foresight; partial nonanticipativity at selected decision epochs with explicit scenario equivalence; full adapted tree; optional information-relaxation penalties with proved nonpositive expectation for every admissible policy. For a minimization penalty, uniform E[M] ≤ 0 or equality suffices; an observed average on one fitted policy does not. Use exact rational toy problems and a genuinely branching counterexample where PF value is strictly below the causal optimum, equal observed histories must share action, and an invalid restriction gives false exclusion. Prove the multi-call viability recursion under explicit order of nominal action, call observation, recourse and disturbance; show monotone shrinkage for nested adversary/call sets. Include inclusive-target equality, missing baseline and original-weight controls. Use Lean with pinned sources, dependency/axiom audit and deliberately false statements; distinguish machine-check, statement correspondence and engineering premises. Review existing stochastic programming, information-relaxation duality, robust viability and value-of-computation literature before novelty claims.

## W07-A — Certified causal opportunity screening (critical)

Build a finite storage/compute decision benchmark with a small exact scenario tree, rational data, repeated indistinguishable observed histories, branch probabilities and temporal coupling. Start with W06 regression and separate development seeds, then freeze a fresh holdout of at least 12 independent latent seed families spanning flat saturation, variable price, forecast ambiguity, rare shock/outage and known positive opportunity. Fix the unit, inclusive practical threshold (1% relative or $15/site-day for economic models where applicable; predeclare absolute floor and definition), information schedule, terminal contract and eligible baseline before opening holdouts. Report all strata and missing/infeasible baselines; never drop weight. Avoid revealing scenario identity/future path through unique labels or future-aware forecast construction.

Evaluate PF and progressively tighter partial nonanticipativity lower bounds, full exact causal DP/tree-MILP where tractable, optional valid centered dual/penalty certificates, and a strong feasible common-information baseline. Use independently checked rational lower witnesses/complete DP branch coverage; validate feasible upper witnesses and explicitly account for tolerance. Record L, U0, achievable feasible gain, target exclusions, unresolved, false exclusions, weighted mean ceiling, time/memory (including baseline, model construction, checking and cold start). Compare direct exact tree solve, native solver bounds and stopping policy; preserve threshold-equality controls and all failures. Use direct solve if cheaper. If no positive meaningful bound remains, stop policy development on that contract.

## W07-E — Multi-call reserve viability and activated economics (critical)

Reproduce W06 one-call checks and the seed 69103 hour-4 consecutive downward-call counterexample. Freeze a call contract before evaluation: direction, activation amplitude in internal blocks and AC conversion, call duration, allowed adjacency, at most K calls per recovery window, public clear-window rules, outage and headroom availability, recourse order, command/ramp history, energy floor, terminal stock and protected/optional service. Test K=0,1,2 and a more demanding declared envelope; a guarantee is only for that envelope. Maintain SOC, pending workload and previous command across midnight. The controller must not use the future actual call sequence. Distinguish outage unavailability, nominal standby and delivered response.

Use backward finite-state robust viability (state = SOC, previous action, remaining obligations, activation history/budget and revealed exogenous information as required); independently enumerate small adversarial call trees and verify witnesses for every allowed call and feasible recovery, including forced up/down, consecutive same-direction, alternating direction, insufficient charging recovery, inverter outage, boundary SOC and ramp. Counterexample at one state disqualifies a universal claim. Compare strong three-scenario H12 policy, hardened one-call, multi-call robust/causal policy and exact small-tree optimum where feasible. Compute certified headroom BEFORE optimization. Include actual activated import, degradation, service interruption and recovery costs, not only no-call mark. Test illustrative rainflow cost sensitivity with reoptimization separated from fixed-schedule revaluation. Predeclare reliability contract, cost/service noninferiority, $15/site-day practical target for matched economics, scenario weights and clustered uncertainty. Keep terminal effects, service, energy, activated reserve and compute clocks separate.
