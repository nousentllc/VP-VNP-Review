# W07-A • Causal opportunity under a published decision-time law

Twelve of 15 frozen synthetic site-days had an attainable expected saving below their predeclared economic target against a rolling-policy portfolio. Six required enforcing some common-history decisions to obtain the first exclusion in the staged procedure. Three deliberately steep delayed-price cases had checked causal gains of $321, $325 and $309. Running the exact causal DP directly was quicker in aggregate on this computer.

## Reproduce

Use Python 3.12 with the packages in `requirements.txt` for the full numerical comparison. The archived checker uses only Python's standard library. From this directory, choose a fresh output directory for each rerun.

```sh
python verify.py
python run.py holdout /tmp/crgc-w07-a-replay
python verify.py /tmp/crgc-w07-a-replay
python summarize.py /tmp/crgc-w07-a-replay
python regression/w06_check.py --data regression/input --out /tmp/crgc-w06-a-regression
```

Each holdout run checks `freeze.json` before creating any cases. Changed clocks, Python executable names, environment details and nondeterministic MILP optima among ties can change raw bytes. Monetary inputs and exact rational optima reproduce independently of those fields. The completed run used Python 3.12.9, SciPy 1.18.0 and NumPy 2.2.4 on arm64 macOS. A stored freeze digest and UTC clock establish an internal ordering. There is no third-party timestamp attestation.

## Contract

One site-day consists of six four-hour dispatch epochs, with five stochastic reveals. There are 63 public history nodes and 32 leaves per family. Fix the seed-generated table before dispatch. It provides conditional ordinary import price, firm backup price, ordinary supply limit, protected compute demand, wear and the probability of next period's high mark at each epoch/current mark. A high mark is a public observation, including when economically immaterial. The full transition law is known. The only realized exogenous arguments at dispatch are current/past marks. Each prefix has a publicly issued rational forecast of the probability of the next high mark, expected price and expected ordinary capacity. The forecast is the expectation under the benchmark's announced law. Its real-world estimation cost is outside this contract. A leaf label never enters policy optimization. Histories are strings of marks already observed, with an empty root key. Conditional future paths in planning represent possibilities and cannot identify the realized future.

State additionally carries inventory 0–4 MWh and previous signed command. Signed action −1, 0 or +1 internal MWh must differ by at most one from the preceding command. Initial stock 2, previous command 0, final stock exactly 2 on every path. A charge purchases 1.25 MWh and a discharge serves 1 MWh. Every protected compute load is served, using capped ordinary supply plus unlimited contracted firm backup at its published higher tariff. The model includes procurement and $1 wear per nonzero block. Costs are in dollars per site-day and simulate neither lost-load exposure nor capacity credit. No optional load, reserve sale or workload deferral is modeled here. The deployed controller is deterministic. Under expected additive costs a finite mixture over feasible deterministic policies cannot lower the minimum.

The common-information baseline evaluates idle and four exact branching H12 rolling policies under the known distribution. Artificial-horizon terminal inventory slopes are 0, 11, 24 and 40 dollars/MWh. All policies maintain endpoint reachability, and enforce exact inventory at the true endpoint. Exact feasible replay evaluates every candidate, choosing one before execution. This assumes the public law is known well enough to evaluate the policy portfolio. The costs of generating and checking all five candidates are charged. Each family has design weight 1/15. The practical target is a saving at least max($15,1% of absolute baseline cost). A stop requires a strictly smaller ceiling.

## Computation and evidence

- `design.json`, `freeze.json` and `model.py` fix the independent-seed families, costs, chronology, threshold, comparator and arm order. The freeze followed the W06 regression and separate development trials. Each family was generated using a fresh locally seeded PRNG. Sharing a designed stratum and mechanism prevents treating the 15 cases as a field sample.
- `results/records.json` holds the raw baseline policies and costs for portfolio members, policy and MILP witnesses, individual clocks, numeric solver records, actual input tables, issued forecasts, path weights and suffix probes. It contains four records per family. Only shadow records include the full input contract. The data include all 480 original weighted leaves. `results/families.csv` and `results/summary.json` give exact per-family and aggregated values. `summarize.py` produces the descriptive summary after execution.
- The first bound is a separable hourly floor. With a uniform positive ordinary tariff, total compulsory gross imports cover total protected service when terminal stock equals initial stock; conversion losses, wear and any firm premium are nonnegative. Otherwise drop storage coupling and price the best physically possible single action for each observation. PF discloses every future mark at the root. Prefix-1 and prefix-3 impose common decision histories for the first one and three epochs respectively, then disclose the remaining path. Any feasible adapted policy embeds by assigning its original actions at every information descendant. Expected cost, physical constraints, terminal obligations and probabilities remain identical.
- For the penalized PF arm, choose a child potential equal to its cheapest feasible one-epoch stage cost plus 11(2−ending SOC), with exact recoverability. For a realized child add conditional mean potential minus realized potential at the reached state. The original probability is conditional on the current public mark. The offset depends only on the candidate parent action and its resulting physical state. The independent arithmetic checker verifies zero conditional drift at every allowed post-action state/child pair, including off-baseline decisions; iterative conditioning gives zero expected penalty for every adapted policy. A pathwise optimal penalized anticipative schedule therefore yields an expected lower bound. It can be weaker than unpenalized PF. The stage uses the best verified floor seen so far. [Brown, Smith & Sun (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.1090.0796)
- Exact causal DP recombines public histories with identical epoch, current mark, inventory and previous command. Stage data, child probabilities and feasible continuations depend only on these quantities. The independent checker keeps distinct histories, enumerates every feasible action and both children at original masses, and uses independently rebuilt terminal reachability. `results/proof_*.json.gz` contain complete reached causal Bellman action tables (weighted Q and branch masses) and the pair of exact penalty residuals for each eligible state. Every witness is evaluated against native exact physics on all leaves. The separate native extensive-form MILP has 63 nodes with binary charge/discharge and linear inventory and supply balance. The SciPy/HiGHS numeric dual remains a numerical comparison. Both native and exact causal policies are independently checked in original integer units. [SciPy milp](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.milp.html)
- `independent.py`, `verify.py` and `results/verification.json` cover 7,845 causal Bellman rows and 1,515 distinct local centering pairs. `results/controls.json` contains a branching counterexample where PF costs 5, exact causal preparation costs 10 and doing nothing costs 25. The exact gain of 15 meets the inclusive target. Restricting the relaxed search to doing nothing, or using an action-dependent penalty empirically centered only at that policy, falsely excludes it. A leaf-specific action at the same history/state fails the nonanticipativity check. Missing baseline weight remains unknown in a separate two-family control. Every confirmatory baseline was eligible.
- Separate cold processes include input generation, five portfolio candidates, checking, imports, JSON IPC, models, solves and verification; the campaign clock in `results/run.json` also includes shadow trials, proof serialization and writing shared outputs up to the final receipt. Arm/seed order rotates deterministically, with one cold observation per arm/family. The native arm checks its feasible incumbent while the independently exact proof comes from the shadow DP. An additional launch of the standalone read-only checker is a delivery audit. Timing cannot establish speedup for unsampled hosts, solvers, horizons or uncertainty structures.

## Earlier trials and limitations

The original pre-freeze development experiment used a single H12 policy with slope 11. It introduced unnecessary cycling on the flat development day and inflated apparent opportunity. The archived first development panel retains those outcomes (`development/initial`). Before freezing, we replaced it with the ex ante five-policy portfolio and moved the intentionally positive test spike beyond the inventory slopes to make a planted feasible alternative observable against that stronger benchmark. The original positive tariff sequence was [0,2,11,13,peak,peak+1], firm price 38+4z. The revised positive sequence is [10,12,25,30,55+peak,57+peak], firm price 110. All nonpositive-regime generation was unchanged. `development/final` contains the separate pilot on revised assumptions. Later additions to proof serialization and output checking preceded the freeze. Confirmatory runs use the frozen code. The holdout was never used to pick these alternatives. `regression/w06-regression.log` records the independent W06 replay of 140 eligible case/objectives and eight controls. Source and exact archived regression inputs are included.

Flat and rare-outage families have zero exact opportunity against this portfolio. The Penalty tier never creates an additional first stop and is strictly looser than PF on one ambiguous family. Direct DP beats the staged method in the paired cold clock on nine families and in aggregate. Only the constructed delayed tariff regime supports a target-sized improvement. Those gains depend on the assumed extremity and timing of the tariffs and the finite-horizon baseline. Independent review should test price-law estimation, longer horizons and stronger portfolio continuations out of sample before giving any field interpretation.

A post-freeze supplemental check independently recreated all five portfolio candidates by replaying policies on reversed leaf order, enumerating endpoint reachability directly in command coordinates and reconstructing the rolling horizon on unrecombined public histories. It recovered all 75 candidate expectations, 15 pre-dispatch winners and 945 selected decision-node actions. Run `python audit_baseline.py`. The recorded result is `results/baseline_supplement.json`; these computations receive no credit in the staged timing.

Run `python development/replay.py` to recover the archived baseline policies and every lower value for both five-family pilot panels using their stored ex ante tables. The initial single-slope panel and its comparator failure remain separately identifiable.
