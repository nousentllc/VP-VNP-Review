# Whole-panel operational sensitivity

The primary two-lane execution experienced a series of supervisor terminations before the worker signalled readiness. Many required imports took longer than the prespecified 15-second startup guard. The host was heavily shared. This prompted a secondary run designed after seeing those failures. Its output is never substituted into the primary panel. The replay includes every date, horizon and arm in the same rotation with one active UC worker and a 180-second allowance for Python startup. The numerical worker, 45-second budget from case parsing through validation, independent check, solver settings, candidate shares and 45.25-second post-start guard are identical. Host contention is observational. Success on the replay would not isolate the causal contribution of any one operational change.
