\sect{W04-B \quad Exact thresholds and bounded regret}
Let $X,Y$ consist of feasible points with real costs $J,K$. Write $A_J(t)$ for $\exists x\in X:J(x)\le t$. Suppose $p:X\to Y$ and $f:Y\to X$ satisfy $K(p(x))\le J(x)$ and $J(f(y))\le K(y)$. Applying the appropriate map to each witness gives $A_J(t)\iff A_K(t)$ for every real $t$, including the empty-domain case. A many-to-one projection can satisfy these hypotheses. Suppose a full feasible lift $x$ has been verified for one attained reduced minimizer $\hat y$, with $J(x)\le K(\hat y)$. Retaining the projection inequality gives, for every full $u$,
\[
 J(x)\le K(\hat y)\le K(p(u))\le J(u).
\]
This proves attainment and equality for that candidate. Equal infima by themselves do not give attainment. For example, $(0,\infty)$ and $[0,\infty)$ with identity cost both have infimum zero, but only the latter attains zero. A reduced optimizer may also encode an interface value outside the projection's range even when all costs are equal.

\sub{Approximation and the price of a partial lift}
Assume nonempty domains with cost ranges bounded below. Let $j=\inf J(X)$, $k=\inf K(Y)$, $K(p(x))\le J(x)+\alpha$, and $J(f(y))\le K(y)+\beta$. Taking infima gives
\[
 k-\alpha\le j\le k+\beta.
\]
For any returned $\hat y$ with $K(\hat y)\le k+\epsilon$, a validated $\hat x$ satisfying $J(\hat x)\le K(\hat y)+\beta$ has $0\le J(\hat x)-j\le\alpha+\beta+\epsilon$. This guarantee needs a lift only at $\hat y$, together with its reduced $\epsilon$-suboptimality certificate and the uniform projection bound. Claiming the full sandwich still requires control in both directions. An empirical panel cannot establish a uniform projection bound. With independent evidence that $L\le j$, a validated $U$ supplies $U-j\le U-L$. Nested inner sets preserve feasibility of old threshold witnesses. A bounded solver can return a worse candidate, so incumbents must be explicitly retained.

\sub{Constructive scope and counterexamples}
The W03 storage instance needs identically constrained integer states, positive device count, label-compatible balanced endpoints, aggregate-only external constraints and fees, and a nonnegative throughput price. Its balanced lift preserves the aggregate and realizes the aggregate movement cost. With positive common fixed shares and distinct positive round-trip efficiencies, exact cyclic endpoints force zero throughput. Positive terminal tolerance permits some cycling. Losses, asymmetric endpoints, or a dispatch history absent from the reduced state require fresh arguments.

Lean 4.30.0-rc2 checked 32 new declarations and the historical 16 inspected results. Eight new false statements failed with separate constructive refutations. Nine prebuilt dependency checkouts matched the pinned revisions. Transitive axiom inspection reported only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} or a subset. Compiler and dependency binaries remain in the trusted base.
An exact finite audit evaluated 1,296 map/objective combinations and 2,592 candidates. The separately identified two-point construction attains the bound $\alpha+\beta+\epsilon$ for every nonnegative triple. Thus an improvement in the universal constant requires additional structural assumptions. Neither algebraic-complexity assumptions nor a solver-efficiency guarantee enter these results.

\sect{W04-E \quad Inventory value and the prediction horizon}
A 35 MW / 180 MWh store supports a fixed facility and flexible computing load. The 100 MW import cap, import ramp, losses, reserve headroom, state floor, and terminal recharge apply to all policies. For each issue time we froze a single forecast of up to 12 hours ending at the day boundary and took prefixes for the three- and six-hour decisions. Current conditions and the next hour's outage are explicitly disclosed. The shared reserve calendar can also signal stress earlier. Each scenario day is simulated chronologically. The 24 seeds supply 72 counterfactual days.

Continuation first minimizes modeled unserved energy over the window, then minimizes end-of-window shortfall relative to a precautionary target, and then minimizes throughput. A deterministic tie-break fixes the first action. The target adds the W03 training 75th percentile of the maximum positive prefix of net energy draw in the following six hours, with charging credited and bins set by the window end, to the physical floor at the forecast-window end, caps the result at 180 MWh, and adds no extra inventory at the day boundary. Its calibration omits some ramp and uncertainty interactions. The no-continuation arm shares the remaining priorities. The forecast rule receives the same horizon prefix. Perfect foresight provides an offline service reference.

\sect{W04-C \quad Exact elimination in static AC power flow optimization}
On a generator bus choose the online unit with the largest sum of real and reactive power ranges. From the bus balances substitute its outputs by the complex injection required by voltage, load and shunts minus the outputs of the other units. The original cost and limits are evaluated at the reconstructed $P,Q$. Feasible points project and lift exactly on the common implemented model. Its extra individual angle boxes $|\theta_i|\le 2\pi$ are provably redundant by path lengths at 57 and 118 buses. Redundancy at 300 and 1,354 buses is open. Every saved angle was within 0.865 radians. This static, single-reference formulation retains both branch-end ratings, charging, transformer taps and phase shifts, voltage and angle bounds. Other dynamics or security contingencies would require their own constraints and proof.

All arms use automatic sparse derivatives with the W03-selected IPOPT configuration and a matched DC voltage guess. Full DC uses dispatch-based generation initialization. Full matched receives the generator values reconstructed at the same initial voltages (the solver can push an infeasible starting point into the box). All eliminates one pivot at every generator bus. Selective eliminates only at incident degree $\le3$. Numerical scoring includes model building and independent reconstruction. Differences in the active inequalities, derivative sparsity and barrier search can therefore survive an algebraically exact substitution.

\sect{W04-D \quad Temporary grouping for unit commitment}
PGLib-UC supplies two RTS-GMLC dates with 73 thermal units each and a California date with 610. Each is solved over its initial 12 hours and over all 48. Prefixes retain initial commitment, elapsed on/off time and initial output; they lack obligations beyond their truncated horizon. The model retains individual startup categories, operating costs, output limits, minimum times, ramps, reserves and renewable availability. It has systemwide balance without a transmission or contingency model.

\sect{What an independent review can establish}
\sub{Two findings outside the main comparisons}
\key{Sharpness of the abstract regret bound.} Take two feasible points in each space. Set full costs to $0,\alpha+\beta+\epsilon$ and reduced costs to $\alpha,\alpha+\epsilon$, with all errors nonnegative. Project both full points onto the first reduced point and lift by matching labels. Both transfer inequalities hold. Returning the second reduced point gives regret exactly $\alpha+\beta+\epsilon$. The construction, Lean declaration and rational tests are separated from the storage theorem in \texttt{B/EXPLORATION.md}.

\key{Forecast accuracy and decisions.} In the 12-hour intervention, issued load leads 6--11 had 5.80 MW mean absolute error against 18.32 MW for a flat extrapolation over 6,696 overlapping issue--target entries. The frozen policy nevertheless delivered less service with the more accurate tail. This finding concerns the specified policy, terminal model and error process. The entries are overlapping predictions, not 6,696 independent days. An optimally informed policy could always ignore additional information. See \texttt{E/EXPLORATORY.md} and \texttt{E/results/tail\_accuracy.csv}.

\sub{Limits on transfer to operations}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Certified battery abstraction.} The generic inequalities and ideal homogeneous lift have precise scopes. Applying them to lossy, heterogeneous devices needs a device-level lift or a uniform distortion proof that includes individual endpoints, history, and all external obligations.
\item \textbf{Operational dispatch and reserves.} The computing load is flexible energy in a simulation. During the specified battery outage the unavailable reserved headroom is tallied as an unmet physical resource. Utility telemetry, sustained reserve delivery, service-level agreement compliance and battery degradation costs were outside the model.
\item \textbf{Network and commitment.} C provides local continuous static solutions. D models chronological commitment without a grid. Neither evaluated security-constrained UC, contingencies, frequency response or capacity credit. Weak numerical lower bounds and conditional timing ratios are insufficient for an optimality or deployment guarantee.
\item \textbf{Timing and external validity.} Every gate is scored on its own clock. Even verified small numeric residuals imply neither an actuation deadline nor performance throughout a real forecast distribution. A blinded external machine replay with a controlled CPU allocation and an out-of-generator forecast panel would test these remaining dependencies.
\end{itemize}
