# Independent post-freeze review of C and A’s size interpretation

## Elimination and validation

For an eliminated generator at bus i, the P and Q balances determine its active and reactive generation uniquely given bus voltage, branch-end powers, loads, shunts, and the other generators at i. The two original generator output boxes become two bounded expressions on those residuals. Generator cost uses the reconstructed P in MW. The inverse of projection is therefore a feasible lift for the common implemented, static continuous OPF. The eight chosen input files contain ten generator columns, three-coefficient active-power polynomial cost and no piecewise-cost, capability-curve or intertemporal constraints. Each active generator bus has one generator in these files. All quadratic coefficients are exactly zero. The panel tests linear cost after substitution; it cannot measure additional Hessian coupling caused by eliminating generators with nonlinear costs. Multi-generator selection is implemented but this panel gives it no empirical test.

There is a common additional box on every bus angle, |θ| ≤ 2π. Each branch has ±30° angle-difference limits. Shortest paths to the reference prove that the added box is redundant on the 57/118 graphs (maximum hop distance ten/eight); on 300/1,354 the largest distances are 17/16, and 35/104 buses respectively lie more than twelve branches from the reference. Path bounds alone cannot establish redundancy there. The reduction is exact for the common model. Global equivalence of this common angle-box model to every native PGLib feasible trajectory on the large graphs remains unverified. This distinction does not impair a returned solution that independently satisfies the native constraints.

The frozen validator uses branch currents calculated from tap/shift and the line π model, both-end apparent-power limits, complex nodal balance, shunts, individual generation, voltage, angle differences and reference. Its reported balance metric is max absolute P or Q residual (componentwise), although its label uses MVA. The supplemental reviewer implementation parses the .m arrays separately, builds sparse bus-to-branch connection and admittance matrices, and rechecks each saved terminal state. It also explicitly checks finite values and the original code’s additional ±2π angle box. It imports the frozen DC warm-start generator and numerical tolerances only. The code and full counts are in review_check.py and review_verification.json; run from W04 with the pinned environment using `python C/review_check.py --raw C/raw --out /tmp/w04-c-review.json`. Negative controls on a valid state establish rejection of the specific one-MW supply perturbation; a rejected mutation of an already-invalid state adds no sensitivity evidence.

## Starting-point correction

The frozen field reconstructed_start_box_MW_MVAr is computed from p0/q0. In full_matched, the implementation first fills all generator-bus pivots from the AC residual, and the field describes the physical start. In all/low_degree, the implementation drops pivot coordinates from x0 and lifts them later. Its field therefore reports the original DC schedule’s box violation (approximately zero), even when the *lifted* physical start is far outside its boxes. This is a diagnostic metadata error. The optimizer uses the intended reduced variables, reconstruction and bounds. Scores can still be audited directly from final vectors.

## Structure, bounds and public comparator

The derivative probe preserves the explicit structural zeros from CasADi for Jacobian and Lagrangian Hessian patterns, augments the primal/constraint graph with artificial diagonals, counts lower factor entries under reverse Cuthill–McKee ordering, and independently checks that counting routine on small graphs. On 1,354 typical, all-pivot reduces the variable count from 3,228 to 2,708 and leaves all 8,681 rows, while RCM added lower-triangular nonzeros rise from 99,221 to 107,615. The structural Lagrangian Hessian nonzero count remains 19,096 in both formulations. The post-clock factor timer uses a separate SuperLU/COLAMD factorization. The one-norm condition diagnostic uses fixed 1e−6 regularization, the scaled objective and that run’s returned primal/dual point; the norm of the inverse is estimated. Variable bounds, active inequalities, barrier diagonals, scaling, linear solver ordering and numerical pivoting can make IPOPT’s actual Newton system quite different. Diagnose one successful or failed first-repeat probe per arm and case as recorded; do not treat these proxies as five repeated solver factorizations or proof of a cause of end-to-end timing differences. Conditional time ratios lose repeat pairs when either arm misses the deadline or returns a materially different cost. Always pair them with unconditional attainment.

The publisher’s baseline is the fifth table field, AC cost in $/h, in the appropriate typical or API row. It is reported to roughly five significant digits. A price rounding discrepancy can exceed the within-study 1e−5 matching tolerance. Use the publisher’s stated precision for external comparisons, and use unrounded pairwise costs within the experiment. PowerModels.jl 0.19.9, Ipopt.jl 1.4.1 / IPOPT 3.14.4, HSL MA27, dual Intel 2.10 GHz, estimated solve time, omitted Julia JIT: that provenance supports objective cross-checks. A local AC solution and an independent publisher’s rounded local optimum do not certify the global optimum. The W04 dispatch-floor relaxation requires nonnegative conductance/loss assumptions, drops network constraints and uses a numerically padded floating-point support value. ## Independent scope check on A

Recomputing from the raw 1,080 trial rows and checking the per-case differences confirms five practical 2 s wins against direct MILP among the six 12×16 forced-opposition cases, one tie, and six losses against LP repair on those same six. This is a descriptive size stratum of an intentionally adversarial synthetic family. The eight-device forced stratum recorded six practical ties against each comparator at two seconds. On the entire 18-case forced stratum the direct comparator had five wins, eleven ties and two losses for grouping, versus 0/11/7 against LP repair. The seven losses are one small and six large cases. The panel does not support a size-trend, field-frequency, device-count scalability or generic speed claim. The group-start 54 target cases all stopped at the LP; any advantage on those cases is attributable to the LP-first policy. At 250 ms the timing gate removed one matched case vs direct or repair, two vs the no-transfer ablation. All 18 have two-second comparisons.
