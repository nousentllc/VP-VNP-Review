# Additional diagnostic · separating a longer model from a longer load forecast

**Rationale.** Comparing H6 and H12 directly changes both the information supplied and the decision horizon. The preregistered tail intervention keeps the twelve-hour optimization, terminal estimator, time-indexed floors and availability model fixed. It replaces load forecasts beyond the sixth entry with the sixth value held constant. Simulated actual loads and current observations are untouched. This is a controlled input intervention. A constant forecast is one explicit comparator, and the effect depends on that imputation.

**Fresh results.** With identical twelve-hour continuation control, supplying the actual issued load-forecast tail changed unserved energy by +8.344 MWh/day relative to constant-tail imputation (24-day interval [+6.146, +10.493]). The imputed H12 policy differed from the ordinary H6 continuation policy by -8.393 MWh/day ([-12.261, -4.942]). That second contrast includes model length, end-of-window target date, and the assumption specifying the tail. All effects are equal-weighted by day and regime. Individual differences are in results/paired_days.csv.

**Post hoc forecast scoring.** The inferior service under the issued tail raised a distinct question about forecast accuracy. Across 6,696 issue/target pairs with lead 6–11, the issued tail had 5.803 MW absolute error on average versus 18.317 MW for a flat tail. RMSE was 8.164 versus 25.626 MW. These are descriptive scores on overlapping targets and shared days. The issued point forecast was more accurate by both metrics, yet using that forecast in this particular deterministic H12 controller resulted in greater realized shedding. No forecast model or decision policy was retuned. The comparison does not establish a general negative value of information for an optimal stochastic policy. See results/tail_accuracy.csv.

**Separate W03 diagnosis.** The Mac and reviewer Linux trajectories at outage_reserve_864 diverge at zero-based index 11 (hour 12). At that decision, two different admissible actions have the same optimal forecast shedding and cycling cost. The observed difference of 1.898633 MWh in realized service comes entirely from that one choice and its effect on future inventory. Injecting the Linux action on the Mac reproduced all remaining Linux outputs. The handling of equal optima therefore explains the archived discrepancy on these files without attributing it to forecast or physics differences. Both dual simplex and interior point reproduce the new deterministic tie-break on this single day. This does not constitute an independent cross-platform trial of the new campaign.
