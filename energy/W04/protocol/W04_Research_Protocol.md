## W04-A — Grouped primal heuristic vs LP-informed full-MILP warm start (critical)

**Question:** Does a transient shared-mode restriction help an unrestricted solver obtain a *better validated incumbent at a fixed deadline*, beyond a simpler LP-informed repair/warm start? Do not confuse the first improvement to fallback with a matched-quality improvement.

- Reproduce W03-A and its fixed-deadline trajectories. Use unchanged battery dynamics, individual terminal tolerances, ramp and reserve obligations. Develop on W03; hold out six fresh proposed seeds (10403, 10429, 10433, 10501, 10513, 10531) crossed with 4×8, 8×12, 12×16 devices×hours and W03's four cohorts (72 instances if all are constructible); add a clearly separated larger-scale panel if resources permit. The adversarial forced-opposition family is a synthetic mechanism test, not representative frequency. Include a feasible common fallback where demonstrable; do not give an arm extra information about the optimum. Preserve infeasible instances/failed construction separately, do not replace difficult holdout cases after seeing results.
- Mandatory arms: (1) tuned full MILP with common fallback start; (2) full LP + validation; (3) LP-informed repair or sign-fixed solve, then pass the best validated recovered candidate as an actual full-MILP MIP start; (4) LP-informed adaptive grouped candidate generation, then pass the best validated grouped candidate as an actual full-MILP start with *all* individual mode choices restored. A fifth arm replicates W03 sequential grouping if feasible. Baselines share LP access where relevant. A validated grouped candidate is an upper bound, never an unrestricted lower bound. Use the full LP outer bound (record exact rational box evaluation for stored coefficients) and unrestricted solver dual bound with stated numerical scope. Record if the solver actually accepts/usefully processes the MIP start; a setSolution invocation alone does not prove acceptance. Include equivalent preprocessing and start transfer/check overhead for every arm.
- Prespecify the candidate-generation time share(s) on development data; e.g., 10/20/30% of total budget as development-only options, then lock one. Use 2 s end-to-end primary budget, with objective-quality snapshots at 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2000 ms. Rotate arm order, three repeats minimum. Log all validated incumbent and bound events, restricted-stage infeasibility, early candidate source, final source, and solver processing. Do not attribute the final unrestricted incumbent to grouping without a matched ablation. Treat the clock as a hard gate for scoring: late output cannot replace fallback. Prevent overruns where possible, log unavoidable overruns.
- Primary target: W03's U−L ≤ 0.00002 + 0.001 max(1,|U|) at two seconds; compare case-level success (all repeats) and median capped time to target against both full MILP and LP-informed warm start. Co-primary fixed-budget quality: paired median feasible U at prespecified 250 ms and 2 s, with exact raw differences, common fallback, and practical improvement threshold selected on development and frozen before holdout. Secondary: first improvement, primal-quality time integral normalized by common fallback-to-valid-bound interval when positive, time-to-target censoring, memory, presolve, and cost of grouping. Report LP-tight cases separately; when raw LP is admissible and meets target, grouping is unnecessary. Report paired differences and uncertainty by *case*, not timing repeats treated as independent.
- Falsifier: no increased target attainment or practical paired incumbent-quality advantage over LP-informed recovery at matched resources, or improvement lost once group solve, start-transfer and validation overhead are counted. Restrict a positive claim to the actual deadline/regime and distinguish any early-only effect.

## W04-B — Generalized AIPC theorem and certified reconstruction (critical)

**Question:** Which exactness and bounded-loss claims can be proved without assuming that every full trajectory is representable? Define the general mathematical contract independent of VP/VNP and prove the statements before attaching engineering interpretations.

- Start from W03's `formal/Storage.lean`, pinned Lean 4.30.0-rc2 and mathlib `896cc56a395e1615786fac56564a3fe6bfeebcc4`. Rebuild source if possible, report whether dependencies are prebuilt or newly built; preserve versions and axiom inspections. Confirm the theorem statement and dependency closure, not merely a build exit code. Keep W03's balanced labeling and positive weights/efficiency hypotheses.
- Define original feasible set X, objective J, reduced set Y, objective J̃, projection and feasible lift. Prove threshold equivalence (for every real b, existence of a feasible cost ≤ b iff a reduced feasible cost ≤ b) from two cost-nonincreasing maps, including empty sets. Distinguish equality of infima from attainment and from a stronger bijective or trajectory-preserving condition. Prove the weaker optimal-lift condition separately with explicit attainment. Construct a counterexample where optimal values agree but a reduced optimizer fails to lift unless the needed assumption is added.
- For nested inner sets X0 ⊆ X1 ⊆ ... ⊆ X with full model at finite refinement, prove nonincreasing attainable infima or threshold containment; empty inner sets allowed. Distinguish theoretical optimum monotonicity from returned incumbent monotonicity; retaining the best validated incumbent ensures the latter. Given valid unrestricted lower bound L and feasible U, prove nonnegative gap and U−J*≤U−L; refine lower bound or candidate until ε, or return last validated incumbent/fallback. Reaching singleton representation does not guarantee meeting ε before deadline or closing an LP relaxation gap.
- Prove a usable approximate transfer bound: if each full feasible x has projected y with J̃(y)≤J(x)+α and each reduced y has validated lift with J(ψ(y))≤J̃(y)+β, then infima sandwich holds and an ε-suboptimal reduced candidate has full-model regret ≤α+β+ε, under explicit nonempty/bounded-below assumptions. If lift is only partial, require feasibility or conditional certificate on the returned y; do not infer global approximation from a test panel. Optionally derive finite-horizon state abstraction error under a precisely defined transition/cost condition, avoiding unstated Lipschitz claims under equality constraints. Establish which conditions can actually be checked cheaply; no generic algorithmic efficiency theorem follows from these inequalities.
- Reuse W03 proof controls; add false theorem mutations with genuine counterexamples (not just tactic failure): missing lift, unbalanced endpoint labeling, invalid restricted lower bound, unequal efficiencies with positive terminal tolerance, and history omission. Check exact arithmetic witnesses and independent semantic examples. Formalize representative propositions in Lean, log accepted and rejected cases; if unavailable, deliver explicit pen-and-paper derivations, executable rational controls and blocker, not fictitious compilation. Compare prior art: exact lumpability/bisimulation, state aggregation, abstract interpretation/approximate simulation, polyhedral inner/outer flexibility, decomposition and clustering. Separate established elementary facts from new engineering-specific constructive conditions. Review proof sources for possible techniques only; no transfer theorem from VP/VNP claimed.

## W04-C — AC elimination, sparsity, fill-in and conditioning (high)

**Question:** When does exact generator-variable elimination improve actual numerical solve behavior, rather than merely reduce variable counts?

- Reproduce W03-C if feasible. Pin additional PGLib normal/stressed pairs at approximately 57, 118, 300 buses and one materially larger case if resources permit. Select actual available cases and variants before holdout, document exclusions and supported cost/shunt/transformer/branch conventions. A static OPF is not SCOPF. Full sparse tuned IPOPT with exact automatic derivatives is mandatory. Include matched retained-variable DC start (document reconstructed generator start difference) and, where possible, full initial point reconstructed to the same state to isolate start vs elimination. A third structure-aware variant may selectively eliminate only generators satisfying a prespecified sparsity/conditioning criterion selected on development, with exact feasible lifting and preserved cost/limits.
- For unit-coefficient P/Q balance substitutions, show that original constraints and objective are evaluated at the reconstructed generator outputs; retain active/reactive limits and any generator cost/coupling constraints. Record full/reduced variable and row counts, Jacobian/Hessian structural nonzeros, sparsity patterns, KKT graph symbolic fill-in under a named ordering, factorization time if available, numerical conditioning proxy and its definition, iteration count, peak memory, construction and validation time, status, and feasible objective. A sparsity or condition estimate is not automatically the true factorization cost; label instrumented vs proxy results. Include at least five repeats if feasible, same 12 s gate as W03 (or preregister a separate larger-case gate applied uniformly).
- Primary outcomes: independent AC feasibility by deadline and paired feasible objective at matched budget; report timing at comparable feasibility/cost and null results. Validate both branch ends, balance, voltage/angles, generator boxes, transformer/shunt model and all required constraints; perturb returned solutions as negative controls. Use valid relaxation bound if available, not a local solution as global optimum. Include model-building and derivative generation; no unexplained missing cases.
- Falsifier: no practical end-to-end advantage, degraded convergence, increased fill or validation failures that offset variable reduction. Report regime dependence; do not claim generic OPF speedup.

## W04-D — Temporary generator grouping as a UC warm-start heuristic (high)

**Question:** Can a temporary paired commitment furnish a useful feasible incumbent without imposing permanent cost/feasibility restrictions? W03-D's permanent grouping was costlier; this is not a rerun of that claim.

- Use pinned PGLib-UC models and W03 cases as development/reproduction. Select several additional dates/instances across distinct fleet sizes before evaluating outcomes, plus 12/48-hour horizons with explicit initial histories and truncation scope. Retain all original individual constraints: minimum times, ramp, startup categories, reserve, availability, individual costs. UC only unless explicit network and contingencies are implemented and checked.
- Mandatory arms: tuned unrestricted reference, unrestricted LP-informed rounding/repair warm start, temporary paired commitment candidate followed by unrestricted MILP with that feasible candidate as start, and permanent paired restriction as diagnostic. For groups, tie only commitment at candidate stage; restore all individual binaries at full stage. Budget time share on development. Validate every candidate against the unrestricted model; maintain the common fallback when valid and available. If none exists, report no validated action, not a fictitious fallback. Record full-model valid bound and restricted-model bound separately. Check actual acceptance of start.
- Primary end-to-end budget: 45 s, with validated feasible objective/bound/target, time-to-first valid action and objective traces at 5, 15, 30, 45 s. Also record solver overrun; late output not credited. Reserve enough validation time based on development; use a process-level guard if available. Record all no-incumbent outcomes and numerical solver gaps. Compare cost/quality with a predeclared tolerance and practical threshold. Do not add costs across cases. Include full model construction overhead and common hardware.
- Falsifier: grouped start fails to improve validated deadline attainment or objective quality versus LP-informed recovery, or its restriction/generation cost offsets benefit. Do not claim commercial savings or SCUC superiority.

## W04-E — Terminal value, precautionary energy and horizon ablations (critical)

**Question:** Did the six-hour rolling optimizer lose because it undervalued energy beyond the horizon, because the horizon was short, or because of a different policy feature? Separate these mechanisms. The W03 forecast-rule advantage was measured on constructed days and is not a site-level estimate.

- Reproduce W03 E exactly on the original frozen forecasts; isolate the `outage_reserve_864` cross-platform discrepancy. Pin deterministic tie-breaking across stage objectives (first minimize forecast unserved load, then explicit reserve/continuation treatment per arm, then throughput, then fixed lexicographic action/state order or a documented numerically safe deterministic rule). Compare tie-break variants on development, not held-out. Do not replace the primary unserved objective with a penalty that unintentionally sacrifices service without explicit design. Preserve original W03 results and report fresh results separately.
- Use W03 seeds for development and a fresh preregistered set of at least 24 latent days (e.g., 11001–11024, if resources allow), each with ordinary, biased and outage/reserve variants. Generate and freeze **one shared issue-time maximum-horizon forecast** per day before running any controller. Compare shorter horizons as truncations, not different forecasts; preserve the one-hour outage disclosure and current observation. For the pure horizon ablation distinguish availability of additional future forecast information from the policy choice to use it. All matched comparisons within a horizon must see identical forecasts and constraints. Include regime stratification and dependence across three variants of a latent day. Preserve a feasible common fallback or report failure.
- Mandatory policies: forecast-aware threshold, W03 no-continuation rolling optimizer at H=6, same H=6 with a prespecified precautionary terminal target or soft terminal continuation-value term, and horizon/terminal-value factorial H in {3,6,12} × {none, continuation}. Keep reserve floors and actual service/safety requirements unchanged; a stricter terminal constraint is a policy restriction and must be attributed as such. A terminal-value term may be a calibrated piecewise-linear value of SOC or a service-risk proxy; develop only on training cases and bound/calibrate against empirical conditional future deficits, not realized holdout hindsight. Optionally separate a forecast rule with/without precautionary target to identify its role. Perfect foresight only offline reference; report its objective and physical scope. Use same site model (cap, ramp, 35 MW/180 MWh, reserve, losses, fixed noncurtailable facility load) or explicitly preregister a separate hardware stress arm.
- Primary outcome: total flexible unserved MWh with hard feasibility/terminal/reserve compliance. Secondary: useful served energy (not jobs), losses, throughput/wear proxy, peak import, outage-induced unavailable headroom, fallback, objective and latency. Report paired effects by latent day with uncertainty clustered at day seed; do not count repeated regimes as independent days. Compare service at like reliability; lower loss achieved through more shedding is not an efficiency victory. Report Pareto tradeoffs and runtime; no favorable aggregation of incompatible units. Record all hourly actions, issued forecasts and terminal values; check every action and deterministic reproduction, including differences across solvers if tested.
- Falsifier: no service benefit versus the strong forecast-aware threshold on new days after matched information, physically valid reserve/recharge constraints, continuation-value attribution and latency. If longer horizon helps only through extra information, attribute it separately; if terminal policy helps, isolate its effect against the identical H=6 baseline. If no positive result, retire the tested optimizer mechanism rather than searching indefinitely for favorable days.
